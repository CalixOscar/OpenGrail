# Theosophy

## Historical context

Helena Blavatsky, Henry Steel Olcott, and associates founded the Theosophical Society in New York in 1875. Its center later moved to India, where members engaged Buddhist and Hindu reformers as well as anticolonial and educational projects. Successor organizations and disputes developed around leadership, revelation, and expected world teachers.

## Distinctives and comparative notes

Theosophy teaches spiritual evolution, karma, rebirth, hidden adepts, layered human constitution, and an ancient wisdom underlying religions. Its creative use of Sanskrit and Buddhist vocabulary helped popularize Asian traditions in Euro-American settings while often transforming them through occult and racial-evolutionary frameworks. Historical influence is clear; theological claims about primordial wisdom or masters remain confessional rather than academically established.
