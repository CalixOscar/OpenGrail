# Dudeism

## Historical context

Oliver Benjamin founded the Church of the Latter-Day Dude in 2005 after interpreting the film character Jeffrey “the Dude” Lebowski as a model of easygoing wisdom. Online ordination, essays, gatherings, and adapted ceremonies created an international participatory movement.

## Distinctives and comparative notes

Dudeist writing commends patience, modest pleasure, friendship, and resistance to frantic striving, often comparing these themes with Daoism and Epicureanism. Such comparisons are interpretive affinities, not historical transmission from ancient schools to the film-based movement.
