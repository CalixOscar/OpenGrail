# Church of the SubGenius

## Historical context

Ivan Stang and Philo Drummond began distributing SubGenius materials around 1979. Mail art, zines, radio, performance, “devivals,” and early internet culture expanded its mythology of the salesman-prophet “Bob” Dobbs and an alien conspiracy.

## Distinctives and comparative notes

“Slack” names liberation from work, normality, and imposed expectations. The church deliberately mixes parody, conspiracy tropes, occult references, and communal ritual. Although much is comic, participants have sustained institutions and gatherings for decades.
