# Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn

## Historical context

William Wynn Westcott, Samuel Liddell MacGregor Mathers, and William Robert Woodman founded the Golden Dawn in London in 1888. Its temples admitted women and men and attracted figures including Florence Farr, W. B. Yeats, and Aleister Crowley. Leadership conflicts fractured the original order around 1900, but successor groups transmitted its system.

## Distinctives and comparative notes

The curriculum aligned initiation grades with a Kabbalistic Tree of Life and combined ritual magic, tarot, astrology, alchemy, and visionary work. Much contemporary ceremonial magic descends from Golden Dawn publications and offshoots. Modern groups using the name do not necessarily share one institutional succession.
