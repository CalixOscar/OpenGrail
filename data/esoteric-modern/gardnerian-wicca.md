# Gardnerian Wicca

## Historical context

Gerald Gardner publicized Wicca in 1950s Britain after the repeal of laws against witchcraft claims. Doreen Valiente and other early initiates helped shape the liturgy. Covens transmitted initiation lineages through Britain, North America, and beyond.

## Distinctives and comparative notes

Gardnerian practice commonly includes three initiatory degrees, ritual nudity in some covens, a Goddess and Horned God, seasonal festivals, and oath-bound material. Authority is decentralized among autonomous covens. Historical evidence supports a modern synthesis rather than Gardner's claim of direct survival from a prehistoric witch cult.
