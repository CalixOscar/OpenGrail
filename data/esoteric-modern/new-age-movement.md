# New Age Movement

## Historical context

The New Age milieu took recognizable form in the later twentieth century through alternative religious communities, metaphysical bookstores, seminars, channeling networks, holistic health, and mass-market publishing. It has no single founder, institution, or creed. Earlier Theosophy, metaphysical religion, Spiritualism, counterculture, human-potential movements, and selective appropriations of Asian traditions all contributed.

## Distinctives and comparative notes

Recurring themes include inner divinity, personal growth, energetic healing, reincarnation, channeling, planetary transformation, and the harmonization of science and spirituality. Participants often combine practices individually. The category is porous and sometimes imposed by observers. Claims involving quantum physics, lost civilizations, or Indigenous identity require separate evaluation rather than receiving credibility from their inclusion in a spiritual marketplace.
