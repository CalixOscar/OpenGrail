# Fiat Lux

## Historical context

The *Geistchristliche Gemeinschaft Fiat Lux* (Order Fiat Lux, Latin for "Let there be light") was founded in 1980 in the Black Forest region of southern Germany by Erika Bertschinger-Eicke (1929–2019), who adopted the spiritual name "Uriella" (God's Light). Bertschinger-Eicke stated that following a 1973 horse-riding accident and subsequent near-death experience, she received the spiritual gift of clairvoyance and began channeling messages directly from Jesus Christ, the Virgin Mary, and various archangels.

In 1980, after the death of her second husband, Uriella established the spiritual center of Fiat Lux in the village of Ibach near St. Blasien in Baden-Württemberg, Germany, alongside a sanctuary in Schwellbrunn, Switzerland. The community grew to several hundred resident members and thousands of European sympathizers during the 1980s and 1990s. Uriella operated the "Heiligtum Jesu Christi," where she conducted public healing rituals and distributed "Athrum water"—water magnetized and blessed through trance ceremonies.

The movement attracted substantial media coverage and legal scrutiny in Germany and Switzerland throughout the late 1990s, particularly regarding tax issues, the unlicensed practice of medicine, and failed apocalyptic prophecies predicting global catastrophes in 1998. Following Uriella's death in February 2019, the community continued in diminished numbers under the leadership of her husband, Eberhard Eicke ("Iffan"), functioning primarily as a small, quiet devotional circle.

## Distinctives and comparative notes

In the sociological taxonomy of modern European new religious movements (such as the documentation by Georg Schmid and Inform), Fiat Lux represents a notable example of the syncretism between conservative Roman Catholic devotional forms and post-war UFO contactee eschatology.

The group's liturgical life prominently displayed Catholic aesthetic elements: members wore white nun-like robes and habits, venerated statues of the Virgin Mary of Fátima, and prayed the Rosary. However, these traditional forms were integrated into an apocalyptic cosmology. Uriella taught that the Earth was undergoing an energetic shift resulting from human sin and negative astral emissions. During the impending three days of darkness and cosmic purification, she prophesied that an extraterrestrial fleet commanded by Jesus Christ would descend in spaceships to evacuate the chosen believers to safety.
