# Heaven's Gate

## Historical context

Bonnie Nettles and Marshall Applewhite formed a disciplined communal movement in the 1970s, teaching that members could transform beyond the human level. After Nettles died, Applewhite increasingly emphasized leaving the body. Thirty-nine members died by suicide in California in March 1997 while expecting transition to a spacecraft associated with comet Hale–Bopp.

## Distinctives and comparative notes

The group reinterpreted Jesus, apocalypticism, extraterrestrials, celibacy, and bodily renunciation within a technological cosmology. Its deaths require direct, non-romantic description, but they should not be generalized to all UFO religions or new religious movements.
