# Modern Luciferianism

## Historical context

Modern Luciferian identities grew from Romantic reinterpretations of the rebellious light-bringer, occult literature, and twentieth-century left-hand-path organizations. Independent authors and small orders developed different theistic, symbolic, and magical forms.

## Distinctives and comparative notes

Lucifer may represent reason, liberation, forbidden knowledge, spiritual ascent, or an actual divine intelligence. Some Luciferians reject the label Satanist while others overlap with it. The minority branch relation reflects this contested family resemblance and the absence of a single Luciferian church.
