# Universe People

## Historical context

The Universe People (Czech: *Vesmírní lidé*, officially the Cosmic People of Light Powers / *Vesmírní lidé sil světla*) was founded in 1997 in the Czech Republic by Ivo A. Benda (born 1961), an engineer from Česká Lípa. In 1997, Benda began collaborating with Miloslava Drsková, a medium who claimed telepathic contact with extraterrestrial civilizations. Benda claimed to have established personal telepathic contact with Ashtar Sheran (the commanding commander of the Great Cosmic Fleet) and Ptaah (a Pleiadian spiritual guide).

Between 1998 and the mid-2000s, Universe People became one of the most publicly visible new religious movements in the Czech Republic and Slovakia. Benda and his associates distributed tens of thousands of books, brightly colored graphic flyers, and leaflets across major Czech and Slovak cities, set up prominent highway billboards, and maintained extensive early multilingual websites. The group held hundreds of public lectures in community halls and civic centers.

While media coverage frequently treated the movement with humor due to its vivid graphic art depicting blond extraterrestrial beings and colorful spaceships, Czech religious studies scholar Zdeněk Vojtíšek documented that the group built a dedicated network of several thousand committed practitioners and sympathizers. In recent years, while public billboard campaigns have declined, the movement maintains an active online presence and digital archive.

## Distinctives and comparative notes

In academic scholarship on UFO religions (notably by Christopher Partridge and James R. Lewis), the Universe People is recognized as a key example of the adaptation of Western contactee mythology into the post-communist Central European religious landscape. The movement filled a spiritual vacuum following the fall of the Iron Curtain, appealing to individuals seeking alternative spirituality that blended scientific terminology with esoteric Christian ethics.

The movement's cosmology divides the universe into a cosmic struggle between the "Forces of Light" (the Cosmic People, who reside in the 5th and higher vibrational dimensions, embody pure love, and practice advanced spiritual technology) and the "Forces of Darkness" (Saurians or Reptilians, negative beings from lower dimensions who manipulate humanity through greed, meat consumption, and materialism).

A central doctrinal focus is the rejection of "chip totalitarism" (*čipová totalita*). Benda teaches that dark forces seek to implement mandatory microchipping of human bodies (equated with the Mark of the Beast in Revelation) to control human consciousness. Those who cultivate positive vibrations, love, and vegetarianism are promised physical or spiritual evacuation onto Ashtar's fleet prior to catastrophic global cleansing.
