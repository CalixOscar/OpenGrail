# Church of All Worlds

## Historical context

Oberon Zell and Lance Christie organized an early form of the Church of All Worlds in 1962, borrowing the name and water-sharing language from Robert Heinlein's novel *Stranger in a Strange Land*. It became an influential American Neo-Pagan institution through intentional community and *Green Egg* magazine.

## Distinctives and comparative notes

CAW has emphasized nature religion, pluralism, sacred sexuality, environmental concern, and creative ritual. It is a real religious organization inspired partly by fiction, not merely a fandom. Its dual placement reflects both Neo-Pagan identity and the scholarly category of fiction-derived religion.
