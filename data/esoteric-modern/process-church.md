# Process Church of the Final Judgment

## Historical context

Mary Ann and Robert de Grimston formed the Process in 1960s London after leaving Scientology. It developed communes, striking black-clad public imagery, magazines, and chapters in Europe and North America. The organization split and transformed during the 1970s.

## Distinctives and comparative notes

Process theology sought reconciliation among Jehovah, Lucifer, Satan, and Christ within an approaching judgment. Sensational allegations linking the group to unrelated crimes have not been substantiated and should not replace primary evidence. Later successor organizations often rejected or reinterpreted its earlier theology.
