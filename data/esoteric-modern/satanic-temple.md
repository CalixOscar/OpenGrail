# The Satanic Temple

## Historical context

The Satanic Temple appeared in the United States in 2013 and became known through public campaigns concerning religious pluralism, church-state separation, and bodily autonomy. It developed congregations, rituals, legal advocacy, and a formal set of ethical tenets.

## Distinctives and comparative notes

TST is nontheistic: Satan functions as a literary emblem rather than a supernatural being. Its activism is also lived by members as religious identity and community. It differs institutionally and philosophically from the Church of Satan, and neither represents historical accusations of devil worship made against minority groups.
