# Raëlism

## Historical context

Claude Vorilhon, later Raël, founded the movement after reporting an encounter in France in 1973 with an extraterrestrial representative of the Elohim. Publications and missionary organization spread the message internationally. Raëlism participates in the twentieth-century UFO-religion milieu while developing its own rituals, ethics, leadership, and expectation of an embassy for the creators.

## Distinctives and comparative notes

Raëlians interpret biblical and other religious narratives as misunderstood accounts of advanced biotechnology, reject supernatural creation, and promote sensuality, scientific progress, and human cloning. The movement's existence is historically documented; its claims about extraterrestrial creators are not supported by archaeology, genetics, or ancient-language scholarship. The graph therefore marks the reinterpretive claim—not the community itself—as speculative fringe.
