# Alexandrian Wicca

## Historical context

Alex and Maxine Sanders became public faces of British witchcraft in the 1960s and initiated a network of covens later called Alexandrian. The tradition drew substantially on Gardnerian material while developing its own lineages, public style, and ceremonial emphases.

## Distinctives and comparative notes

Alexandrian covens usually use degree initiation, seasonal and lunar rites, polarity symbolism, and ceremonial magic. Practice varies by lineage, and some covens share dual Gardnerian-Alexandrian inheritance. The name marks initiation history more than a centrally governed denomination.
