# Reclaiming Tradition

## Historical context

Reclaiming formed in the San Francisco Bay Area in 1979 around Starhawk, Diane Baker, and collaborators. Camps, public rituals, affinity groups, and activist networks spread its practices internationally while retaining decentralized governance.

## Distinctives and comparative notes

The tradition joins earth-based spirituality, magic, feminism, nonhierarchical process, and political action. Its public Principles of Unity affirm inclusivity and ecological responsibility. Some scholars place Reclaiming within Wicca, while participants may prefer broader witchcraft or Pagan identities; the minority branch certainty preserves that porous boundary.
