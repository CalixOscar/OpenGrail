# Nuwaubian Nation

## Historical context

The Nuwaubian Nation was founded in late 1960s New York City by Dwight York (born 1945), who operated under numerous names and titles, most prominently as As Sayyid Al Imaam Isa Al Haadi Al Mahdi, Chief Black Eagle, and Dr. Malachi Z. York. Originating in Brooklyn around 1967 as the Ansar Pure Sufi, the movement evolved through several distinct ideological and aesthetic phases over four decades.

During the 1970s and 1980s, the group operated as the Ansaaru Allah Community, presenting itself as an orthodox Islamic and Sufi movement whose members wore traditional Sudanese robes, turbans, and veils, operating storefronts and publishing extensive tracts. In the late 1980s, York shifted the group's doctrinal focus toward Black Hebrew Israelite themes, before transitioning in the early 1990s to an Afrocentric ancient Egyptian (*Kemetic*) and extraterrestrial UFO cosmology, adopting the name the United Nuwaubian Nation of Moors.

In 1993, York moved the movement from Brooklyn to a 476-acre rural property in Putnam County near Eatonton, Georgia. There, the Nuwaubians constructed "Tama-Re" (also called the "Egypt of the West"), an elaborate, walled compound featuring life-sized Egyptian pyramids, obelisks, and sphinx statues. The compound hosted annual Saviour's Day festivals drawing thousands of followers from across the United States. Following York's arrest in 2002 and conviction in 2004, the Tama-Re compound was seized and demolished by federal authorities in 2005.

## Distinctives and comparative notes

In the academic study of new religious movements (notably the detailed ethnography by Susan J. Palmer), Nuwaubianism is recognized as one of the most doctrinally fluid and visually inventive religious movements in modern American history. York authored hundreds of illustrated booklets and scrolls covering topics ranging from ancient Sumerian tablets to extraterrestrial genetic engineering.

The movement's core theology, termed "Right Knowledge" (*Nuwaupu*), asserts that Black people (*Nubians*) are the original supreme beings of the universe, possessing nine ether hair and divine genetic heritage from extraterrestrial beings called the Annunaqi (from the nineteen-galaxy star system of Illyuwn and the craft Nibiru). York taught that white people were genetically engineered 6,000 years ago by an evil scientist named Yakaan, and that a grand spacecraft would arrive to rescue the 144,000 chosen Nuwaubians before a global cosmic realignment.

## Documented harms and controversies

In May 2002, federal and local law enforcement authorities executed a massive search warrant on the Tama-Re compound in Putnam County, Georgia, arresting Dwight York on multiple state and federal charges:

- **Federal Racketeering and Child Molestation Conviction (2004)**: In January 2004, following a high-profile trial in the United States District Court for the Middle District of Georgia, Dwight York was convicted on multiple federal counts, including racketeering (RICO), transporting minors across state lines for sexual purposes, and financial structuring violations. Evidence and testimony from numerous former child members of the commune detailed extensive, systematic sexual abuse spanning decades.
- **Sentencing and Incarceration**: In April 2004, York was sentenced to 135 years in federal prison. He is currently incarcerated at ADX Florence, the federal supermax facility in Colorado.
- **Demolition of Tama-Re (2005)**: Following the forfeiture of the Eatonton property to satisfy federal restitution and seizure orders, the Tama-Re compound was sold and demolished in June 2005.

While York remains incarcerated, small factions of loyal Nuwaubian adherents continue to advocate for his release and maintain online study groups disseminating his voluminous writings.
