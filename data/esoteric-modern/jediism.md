# Jediism

## Historical context

Jedi identification appeared among fans and online communities in the late twentieth century, then gained visibility through religion responses in several national censuses beginning in 2001. Organizations developed training programs, ethical codes, clergy roles, and meditation practices with varying degrees of seriousness and relation to fandom.

## Distinctives and comparative notes

Jediists commonly interpret the Force as a spiritual, ethical, psychological, or metaphorical principle and cultivate service, self-discipline, and emotional balance. Star Wars itself drew eclectically on Asian religions, monastic imagery, mythology, and film traditions; direct lineage claims to Buddhism or Daoism would therefore be misleading.
