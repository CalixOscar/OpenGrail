# Parody & Fiction-Derived Religions

## Historical context

Modern mass media, counterculture, legal debates about religion, and online community produced movements that openly draw on satire or fictional worlds. Some began as protests or jokes but developed durable identities, rituals, ethics, organizations, and sincere participants. Scholars use several overlapping labels for this field.

## Distinctives and comparative notes

Parody, play, and religious seriousness are not mutually exclusive. A movement may mock institutional privilege while also providing community and a way of life. The umbrella is analytical: Pastafarians, Jediists, Discordians, and Dudeists do not belong to one organization or necessarily understand “belief” in the same way.
