# Thelema

## Historical context

Aleister Crowley founded Thelema after the 1904 reception of *The Book of the Law*, which he attributed to a discarnate intelligence named Aiwass. Crowley reworked ceremonial magic, yoga, mysticism, and initiatory orders into a new religious system. Thelemic communities now include several organizations and unaffiliated practitioners.

## Distinctives and comparative notes

The maxim “Do what thou wilt” refers within Thelema to discovering and enacting True Will, not simply following impulse. Ritual magic, meditation, graded initiation, sacred sexuality, and devotion to figures such as Nuit, Hadit, and Ra-Hoor-Khuit may feature. Claims about revelation are theological; the movement's historical debt to the Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn and broader esotericism is documented.
