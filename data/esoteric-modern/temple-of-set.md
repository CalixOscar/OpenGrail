# Temple of Set

## Historical context

Michael Aquino and associates formed the Temple of Set in 1975 after leaving the Church of Satan. The organization developed a graded initiatory system drawing on ceremonial magic, Western esotericism, and a reinterpretation of the Egyptian deity Set. Its internal schools, called Orders, pursue specialized forms of study and practice.

## Distinctives and comparative notes

Setians generally affirm Set as an objective metaphysical intelligence and pursue *xeper*, self-directed becoming or coming into being. This distinguishes the Temple's theology from the Church of Satan's symbolic atheism. Its use of Egyptian names is a modern esoteric reception, not continuity with ancient Egyptian priesthood, and should be differentiated from reconstructionist Kemetism.
