# UFO Religions

## Historical context

After the flying-saucer reports of the late 1940s, contactees and new religious movements combined space-age imagery with Theosophy, Christianity, millennial expectation, channeling, and evolutionary narratives. The category includes very different organizations and is used analytically rather than as a shared self-name.

## Distinctives and comparative notes

Movements may describe extraterrestrials as creators, ascended teachers, rescuers, judges, or fellow cosmic beings. Documenting such beliefs does not provide scientific evidence for alien contact. The family includes communal religions, loose channeling networks, and groups whose histories ended in violence, making careful case-by-case treatment essential.
