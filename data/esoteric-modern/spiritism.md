# Spiritism

## Historical context

French educator Hippolyte Léon Denizard Rivail, writing as Allan Kardec, organized reports of mediumistic communication into a systematic doctrine beginning in 1857. Spiritism spread especially strongly in Brazil, where associations, charities, healing practices, and publishing networks became major institutions.

## Distinctives and comparative notes

Spiritists teach survival after death, repeated incarnation, communication with spirits, moral causation, and progressive education across lives. Kardec distinguished his codified philosophy from the broader Spiritualist séance movement. Academic inclusion records a historical religion; it does not verify paranormal or medical claims.
