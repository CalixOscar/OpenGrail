# Pastafarianism

## Historical context

Bobby Henderson proposed the Flying Spaghetti Monster in a 2005 open letter opposing the presentation of intelligent design alongside evolution in Kansas science classes. Internet participation, public rituals, satire, and legal campaigns for equal treatment turned the conceit into an international movement.

## Distinctives and comparative notes

Pastafarian language includes pirates, colanders, mock commandments, and a deliberately unfalsifiable creator. Participants range from secular activists to people claiming sincere religious identity. Courts and governments have reached different conclusions about recognition; those legal outcomes do not alone determine how adherents understand the movement.
