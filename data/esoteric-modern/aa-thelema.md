# A∴A∴

## Historical context

Aleister Crowley and George Cecil Jones established A∴A∴ after the fragmentation of the Golden Dawn. Its curriculum appeared through *The Equinox* and combined ceremonial magic, yoga, mystical attainment, and systematic personal recordkeeping. Multiple claimant lineages now transmit versions of the system.

## Distinctives and comparative notes

Students advance through grades mapped partly onto the Kabbalistic Tree of Life, usually working through a direct teacher relationship rather than a lodge. The program aims at Knowledge and Conversation of the Holy Guardian Angel and later mystical grades. It is organizationally distinct from O.T.O. despite overlapping Thelemic founders and texts.
