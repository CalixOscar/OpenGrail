# Eckankar

## Historical context

Paul Twitchell publicly established Eckankar in the United States in 1965, presenting it as an ancient path newly revealed for modern seekers. Leadership passed to Darwin Gross and then Harold Klemp. The movement developed initiations, local discussion groups, seminars, and a substantial publishing program, while scholars debated Twitchell's use of earlier Sant Mat and esoteric sources.

## Distinctives and comparative notes

Eckankar teaches the soul's journeys through inner worlds under guidance of the Living ECK Master and emphasizes the divine Light and Sound known as ECK. Practices include chanting *HU*, dream study, and contemplative exercises. Its claimed timeless lineage is a religious teaching; textual and institutional history places the public movement in the twentieth century.
