# Modern Paganism

## Historical context

Modern Pagan religions developed through Romantic nationalism, folklore collecting, occult revival, environmental movements, feminist spirituality, and modern reconstructions of ancient religions. Wicca, Heathenry, Druidry, Hellenic polytheism, Rodnovery, and other paths differ in theology, ritual, and attitudes toward historical reconstruction. There is no universal Pagan authority.

## Distinctives and comparative notes

Many Pagan communities honor multiple deities, seasonal cycles, ancestors, land, ritual reciprocity, or immanent sacred power, though none of these defines every group. Modern revivals are new religious formations using fragmentary historical sources, not unchanged survivals from antiquity. Responsible comparison also distinguishes inclusive reconstruction from racialist appropriations found in some marginal organizations.
