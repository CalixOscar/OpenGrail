# Hermeticism

## Historical context

Greek and Latin Hermetic writings emerged in Roman Egypt, presenting dialogues in which Hermes Trismegistus reveals cosmology, piety, mind, and spiritual rebirth. Technical texts on astrology, alchemy, and magic circulated under the same authority. Renaissance translators mistakenly dated the philosophical works before Moses, giving them exceptional prestige until philology established their late antique setting.

## Distinctives and comparative notes

Hermetic texts describe a divinely ordered cosmos that can be contemplated and, in technical traditions, ritually engaged. Knowledge transforms the knower through reverence and purification. Later “Hermeticism” includes many creative receptions rather than an unchanged secret lineage from Egypt. Comparisons with Gnosticism and Neoplatonism illuminate a shared late antique environment but should preserve their doctrinal differences.
