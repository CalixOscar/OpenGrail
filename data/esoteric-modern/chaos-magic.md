# Chaos Magic

## Historical context

Chaos magic developed in Britain during the late 1970s through writers and practitioners including Peter J. Carroll and Ray Sherwin, with the Illuminates of Thanateros as an early network. Zines, small presses, and later internet communities supported a decentralized international scene.

## Distinctives and comparative notes

Practitioners often treat belief as a temporary instrument, combine methods from different systems, create sigils, and use concentrated states called gnosis. This methodological eclecticism does not mean participants believe nothing; approaches range from psychological experiment to spirit-centered practice. Chaos magic is a current rather than one church or uniform theology.
