# Order of the Solar Temple

## Historical context

The Order of the Solar Temple (*Ordre du Temple Solaire*, OTS) was founded in Geneva, Switzerland, in 1984 by Joseph Di Mambro (1924–1994), a French jeweler and long-time esotericist, and Luc Jouret (1947–1994), a Belgian homeopathic physician and charismatic lecturer. The organization originated through the merger of several esoteric circles, including the Renewed Order of the Temple (*Ordre Rénové du Temple*) and the *Club Amenta*.

During the 1980s, the OTS recruited an affluent, educated membership across Switzerland, France, and Canada, establishing lodges in Geneva, Zurich, and Morin-Heights in Quebec. Jouret delivered popular lectures on holistic health, organic farming, and ecological survival, while Di Mambro managed the group's finances and esoteric core. Within the inner circle (*Synarchie*), Di Mambro staged elaborate theurgical rituals using hidden electronic devices, projections, and mirrors to simulate appearances by the "Masters of the Great White Lodge."

By the early 1990s, the group faced mounting crises: internal dissent following the discovery that the spiritual apparitions were staged, police investigations in Canada into illegal firearm purchases, and deteriorating physical health among the leaders. In response, Di Mambro and Jouret reframed their apocalyptic theology from physical survival into a necessary spiritual "transit" (*le Transit*) to the star Sirius.

## Distinctives and comparative notes

The Order of the Solar Temple represented a modern fusion of French neo-Templar chivalry, Rosicrucian mysticism, Theosophical Ascended Master lore, and radical New Age ecological millenarianism. The group taught that the earth was entering an irredeemable period of environmental destruction, moral decay, and cosmic crisis.

According to OTS doctrine, humanity was divided into gross material beings and an enlightened spiritual elite whose souls originated from higher cosmic dimensions. Di Mambro's daughter Emmanuelle was declared the "Cosmic Child" conceived through an immaculate spiritual conception. When the earthly mission was deemed complete, leaders taught that the physical body had to be shed through fire so the liberated solar soul could travel instantaneously to Sirius to continue its cosmic evolution.

## Documented harms and controversies

Between October 1994 and March 1997, the Order of the Solar Temple carried out a series of four coordinated mass murder-suicides across Switzerland, France, and Canada, resulting in the deaths of 74 individuals:

- **October 1994 (Quebec and Switzerland)**: On 4–5 October 1994, 53 people died. In Morin-Heights, Quebec, five members—including an infant labeled the "Anti-Christ"—were stabbed and suffocated before the chalet was torched. Simultaneously, in the Swiss villages of Cheiry (Fribourg) and Granges-sur-Salvan (Valais), 48 members, including Jouret and Di Mambro, died from bullet wounds, sedative poisoning, and synchronized firebomb explosions.
- **December 1995 (Vercors, France)**: On 15–16 December 1995, sixteen members (including three children and French Olympic skier Édith Bonlieu) were found dead in a star formation in a clearing on the Vercors Plateau near Saint-Pierre-de-Chérennes, having been sedated, shot by two designated members, and set on fire.
- **March 1997 (Saint-Casimir, Quebec)**: On 22 March 1997, five remaining members died in a burned house in Saint-Casimir, Quebec, while three teenagers were permitted to survive.

Academic and judicial investigations confirmed that these events were not uniform voluntary mass suicides, but a mixture of premeditated homicides, coerced executions of skeptics and children, and voluntary suicides by devoted inner-circle believers. Following the final deaths in 1997, the organization ceased all operations.
