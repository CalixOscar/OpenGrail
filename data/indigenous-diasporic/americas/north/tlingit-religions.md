# Tlingit Religious Traditions

## Historical context

Tlingit clans developed complex maritime, diplomatic, artistic, and ceremonial systems in Southeast Alaska and neighboring regions. Russian and American colonialism brought trade, Orthodox and Protestant missions, epidemic loss, property seizure, and bans on potlatch. Tlingit Christians and cultural leaders adapted religious forms while maintaining clan law, memorial responsibility, language, and ownership.

## Distinctives and comparative notes

Sacred and historical property known as *at.óow* can include names, stories, songs, designs, places, and objects held through clan rights. Memorial exchanges restore social balance and affirm relationships between moieties and generations. Public visibility does not cancel ownership: reproducing a crest or story without permission can violate both spiritual responsibility and Indigenous intellectual property.
