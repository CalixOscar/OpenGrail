# Purépecha Religious Traditions

## Historical context

The Purépecha state centered in the Lake Pátzcuaro region remained politically independent of the Mexica empire before Spanish invasion. The colonial *Relación de Michoacán* records valuable but mediated information about rulers, priests, temples, and the deity Curicaueri. Conversion, town reorganization, land struggle, migration, and Indigenous Catholic institutions transformed rather than erased local religion.

## Distinctives and comparative notes

Fire, lakes, mountains, ancestors, communal offices, agricultural cycles, saints, and funerary observances remain locally important. Precolonial reconstruction must distinguish colonial testimony from living practice and avoid treating Purépecha religion as a provincial form of Mexica religion.
