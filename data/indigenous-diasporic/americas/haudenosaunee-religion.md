# Haudenosaunee Religions

## Historical context

The Haudenosaunee Confederacy joins Mohawk, Oneida, Onondaga, Cayuga, Seneca, and later Tuscarora nations through the Great Law of Peace while preserving each nation's councils, clans, language, and ceremonial life. European invasion, missionization, dispossession, forced schooling, and border division transformed communities on both sides of the United States–Canada boundary without dissolving sovereignty.

## Distinctives and comparative notes

The Thanksgiving Address, condolence, longhouse ceremonies, seasonal cycles, clan relations, dreams, and responsibilities to the natural world shape distinct but connected traditions. The nineteenth-century Gaiwiio associated with Handsome Lake renewed Seneca religious life in dialogue and tension with Christianity. Claims that the United States Constitution simply copied the Great Law remain debated; respectful comparison recognizes documented diplomatic contact without reducing Haudenosaunee governance to a single influence claim.
