# Siberian Shamanic Traditions

## Historical context

Siberia contains many peoples and language families, including Evenki, Sakha, Buryat, Khanty, and others, whose ritual traditions cannot be collapsed into one system. Russian imperial rule, Orthodox missions, Soviet persecution, settlement, extractive industry, and post-Soviet revival transformed local institutions. The English word *shaman* ultimately derives from a Tungusic term.

## Distinctives and comparative notes

Depending on community, specialists may heal, retrieve souls, guide the dead, divine, mediate with land masters, or sustain clan relations through song, costume, drum, and offering. Similar techniques do not erase distinct cosmologies or political histories. This node groups a region for navigation while directing interpretation back toward specific peoples rather than an imagined pan-Siberian creed.
