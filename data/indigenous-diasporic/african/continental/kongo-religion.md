# Kongo Religious Traditions

## Historical context

Kongo-speaking societies developed across the lower Congo region before the Kingdom of Kongo’s encounter with Portuguese Christianity in the late fifteenth century. Courts and communities adopted, translated, and contested Christian symbols within existing ideas of sacred authority. Colonial borders, missions, warfare, urbanization, and prophetic movements produced substantial regional variation.

## Distinctives and comparative notes

Kongo cosmologies often distinguish yet connect the lands of living and dead through water, burial, lineage, and cyclical imagery. Specialists work with *minkisi*, empowered assemblages used for healing, protection, oath, or justice. Outsider collections frequently called these objects “fetishes,” a misleading term that detaches them from authorized persons, ingredients, speech, and social obligations.
