# Gbe Vodun Traditions

## Historical context

Vodun institutions developed among Fon, Ewe, and other Gbe-speaking communities through households, towns, kingdoms, priesthoods, and regional exchange. The kingdom of Dahomey reorganized some cults without creating a single permanent system. Colonial rule, Christianity, national religious policy, tourism, and Atlantic relationships have continued to change public forms.

## Distinctives and comparative notes

Specific *vodun* relate to natural forces, craft, illness, kin groups, political authority, and particular places through initiation, possession, offerings, drumming, and divination. Terminology and pantheons vary across language and locality. Haitian Vodou preserves major Gbe inheritances but is a distinct Caribbean religion, so similar names should not imply direct institutional identity.
