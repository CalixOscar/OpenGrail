# San Religious Traditions (Comparative)

## Historical context

“San” covers peoples with different languages and histories across southern Africa. Pastoral expansion, settler violence, dispossession, labor systems, conservation policy, and mission Christianity profoundly disrupted communities. Ethnographic archives and rock art preserve important evidence but were often collected under unequal conditions and must be interpreted with living communities rather than treated as ownerless data.

## Distinctives and comparative notes

In several documented traditions, communal dances generate healing potency, alter consciousness, and repair relationships; stories connect animals, persons, weather, and ancestral time. These patterns are not universal across every San group. The comparative term “shamanism” can illuminate trance healing but can also import a Siberian model, so the graph marks it as a debated parallel rather than a self-description.
