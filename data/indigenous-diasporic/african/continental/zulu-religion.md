# Zulu Religious Traditions

## Historical context

AmaZulu religious life has changed through the rise of the nineteenth-century Zulu kingdom, colonial conquest, migrant labor, apartheid, Christianity, and urbanization. Mission archives often interpreted Indigenous categories through Christian assumptions. Today ancestral rites, healing, churches, and national or royal ceremonies can overlap without producing one uniform identity.

## Distinctives and comparative notes

Ancestors or *amadlozi* remain involved in kinship, well-being, misfortune, and moral obligation. Diviners and healers diagnose disrupted relationships and work through dreams, medicines, and ritual. Terms for a high creator have complex histories and cannot be treated as proof of either pristine monotheism or missionary invention; speakers and contexts matter.
