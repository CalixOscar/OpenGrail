# Hoodoo / African American Conjure

## Historical context

Hoodoo developed among enslaved African Americans and their descendants, especially in the southern United States, through Central and West African knowledge, biblical practice, Indigenous and European materia, and the conditions of racial violence. Emancipation, migration, commercial spiritual-supply shops, folklore collecting, and popular media changed how techniques circulated.

## Distinctives and comparative notes

Rootworkers may use herbs, roots, minerals, personal concerns, prayer, Psalms, amulets, baths, and divination for healing, luck, protection, love, or justice. Hoodoo has often been practiced by Christians and does not require membership in a separate religion. It should not be confused with Haitian Vodou, and commercial “spell” stereotypes obscure its African American history and community ethics.
