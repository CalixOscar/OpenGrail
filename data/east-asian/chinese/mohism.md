# Mohism

## Historical context

Mohism arose during the Warring States period around Mo Di, or Mozi, and organized communities of teachers and technical specialists. Mohist texts preserve ethical essays, political proposals, defenses against siege warfare, and sophisticated discussions of language and reasoning. The organized school declined after the Qin-Han transition, although its writings continued to be transmitted.

## Distinctives and comparative notes

Mohists argued for inclusive or impartial care, appointment by merit, economic restraint, opposition to aggressive war, and conformity to Heaven's morally purposive order. They sharply criticized expensive ritual and graded Confucian partiality. Mohism is often categorized as philosophy, but its appeals to Heaven, spirits, moral retribution, and disciplined community make it relevant to comparative religion without turning it into a church-like institution.
