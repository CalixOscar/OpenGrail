# Zhengyi Daoism

## Historical context

Zhengyi lineages trace authority to the Celestial Masters movement founded in the late Han period and to later ritual traditions consolidated under the “Orthodox Unity” designation. Hereditary and ordained priests served local communities across China and Taiwan, adapting through dynastic patronage, local cults, migration, and modern religious regulation.

## Distinctives and comparative notes

Zhengyi priests commonly marry and work within households rather than permanent monasteries. They conduct offerings, exorcisms, funerals, communal renewals, petitions, and rites that place local gods within Daoist celestial administration. The contrast with Quanzhen monasticism is useful but not absolute: ritual repertoires overlap, and living Daoist communities often cooperate across formal lineage boundaries.
