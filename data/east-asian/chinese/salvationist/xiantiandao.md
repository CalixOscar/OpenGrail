# Xiantiandao

## Historical context

Xiantiandao, the “Way of Former Heaven,” names related late-imperial transmission networks rather than one continuously centralized church. Lineages used patriarchal succession, vegetarian halls, initiation, and revealed teaching to organize followers. Several modern movements emerged from this religious environment, most prominently Yiguandao, while others developed independently in China and Southeast Asian Chinese communities.

## Distinctives and comparative notes

Many lineages teach that souls originate with the Unborn or Eternal Mother and must return from the later-heaven world through moral cultivation and initiation. They often interpret Confucian, Buddhist, and Daoist figures within one salvific history. Because scholars reconstruct a complicated web of names and schisms, the umbrella must not erase the contemporary identity or doctrine of any particular descendant.
