# Luo Teaching

## Historical context

Luo Qing, a former soldier and religious seeker, published the texts later called the *Five Books in Six Volumes* around 1509. Communities inspired by his teaching spread among laborers and boatmen along the Grand Canal and generated multiple lineages rather than one centralized organization. Later groups reinterpreted Luo’s writings within changing Maitreyan and maternal cosmologies.

## Distinctives and comparative notes

The texts teach discovery of an originally pure, unborn condition through personal awakening, ethical practice, and critique of merely external ritual. Subsequent descendants could differ sharply over deities, initiation, clerical authority, and millennial expectation. “Luojiao” is therefore a historical family resemblance; it should not be collapsed into every movement that officials associated with prohibited lay religion.
