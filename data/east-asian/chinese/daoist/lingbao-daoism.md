# Lingbao Daoism

## Historical context

Lingbao scriptures appeared in southern China around the turn of the fifth century, partly associated with Ge Chaofu and earlier Ge-family materials. They combined Celestial Master ritual, Shangqing cosmology, older talismanic traditions, and creatively adapted Buddhist ideas. Lingbao liturgies became central to medieval Daoist communal worship and strongly shaped later ritual repertoires.

## Distinctives and comparative notes

The corpus presents a universal sacred order, elaborate heavens, cycles of cosmic renewal, rites for both living and dead, and salvation extending beyond a narrow initiatory elite. Borrowed Buddhist vocabulary was reworked within Daoist cosmology rather than simply copied. As with Shangqing, Lingbao is best mapped as a historically layered textual-ritual current whose elements persist inside later institutions.
