# Shangqing Daoism

## Historical context

Between 364 and 370, Yang Xi recorded revelations said to come from perfected beings for members of the Xu family near modern Nanjing. Tao Hongjing later collected and ordered this material at Mount Mao, making Shangqing scripture prestigious at southern courts. Its texts and ritual imagery entered the broader Daoist canon and continued within later Maoshan transmission.

## Distinctives and comparative notes

Shangqing practice foregrounded the visualization of deities within the body, nocturnal celestial travel, recitation, dietary discipline, and individual meditation more than the communal rites characteristic of early Celestial Masters. Its revealed heavens and divine biographies created new maps of salvation. Scholars use “school” for a changing textual and initiatory current; it was never a denomination with a single continuous bureaucracy.
