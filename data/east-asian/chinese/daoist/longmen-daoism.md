# Longmen Daoism

## Historical context

Longmen genealogies trace the lineage to Qiu Chuji, a leading disciple in early Quanzhen, but its durable institutional form emerged much later. Wang Changyue’s seventeenth-century public ordinations at the White Cloud Monastery helped standardize precepts and spread Longmen affiliations across China. Numerous local sublineages subsequently claimed the name with varying relationships to central monasteries.

## Distinctives and comparative notes

Longmen transmission combines celibate monastic identity, ethical precepts, liturgy, meditation, and internal alchemy. The lineage’s broad reach makes it internally diverse; affiliation may record ordination genealogy rather than a uniform contemplative method. Presenting it as an unchanged medieval order would confuse retrospective ancestry with the historically documented expansion of late imperial institutions.
