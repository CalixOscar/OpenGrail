# Way of the Celestial Masters

## Historical context

Later tradition dates the movement to Zhang Daoling’s revelation from the deified Laozi in Sichuan in 142 CE. Communities organized households into districts, supported ritual officials through contributions, and used registers, confession, petitions, and communal observances. After political dispersal, Celestial Master institutions changed repeatedly and contributed foundational forms to medieval and modern Daoism.

## Distinctives and comparative notes

The tradition joined moral discipline and communal welfare to a bureaucratic sacred cosmos in which authorized priests communicated with divine administrations. Healing and confession addressed disorder in both bodies and communities. “Five Pecks of Rice,” an early outsider designation based on household contributions, should not be mistaken for the movement’s complete self-understanding; modern Zhengyi inherits important elements without remaining identical to every early community.
