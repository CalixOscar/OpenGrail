# Neo-Confucianism

## Historical context

Neo-Confucian traditions took shape in Song China through thinkers including Zhou Dunyi, the Cheng brothers, Zhu Xi, and Lu Jiuyuan, then spread and diversified in Korea, Japan, and Vietnam. The modern label groups several competing lineages. Zhu Xi's synthesis became especially influential through education and civil-service curricula.

## Distinctives and comparative notes

Neo-Confucian thinkers connected family ethics and public responsibility to accounts of *li* (pattern or principle), *qi* (material force), mind, and the investigation of things. They often criticized Buddhism and Daoism even while answering questions sharpened through centuries of interaction with them. It can function as philosophy, ethical formation, ritual tradition, and cosmological teaching; forcing it into a simple religion-versus-philosophy binary obscures that range.
