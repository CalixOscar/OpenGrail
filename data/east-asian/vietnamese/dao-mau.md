# Đạo Mẫu

## Historical context

Mother-goddess cults have long histories in Vietnam, while the Four Palaces configuration and prominent devotion to Liễu Hạnh took recognizable form in the early modern period. Colonial classification, socialist restriction, market reform, and UNESCO recognition all reshaped public practice. Temples and medium lineages remain locally organized rather than governed by one church.

## Distinctives and comparative notes

The *hầu đồng* possession ceremony presents divine personae through colored robes, dance, offerings, and *chầu văn* music. Devotion connects health, fortune, protection, gendered authority, and the landscapes of heaven, mountains, waters, and earth. Although Daoist and Buddhist vocabulary may appear, Đạo Mẫu is not reducible to either and practitioners differ in how they name its religious status.
