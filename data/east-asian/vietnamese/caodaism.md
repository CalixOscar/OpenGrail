# Cao Đài

## Historical context

Cao Đài was formally established in southern Vietnam in 1926 after spirit-medium sessions conveyed revelations attributed to the Supreme Being. It developed an elaborate ecclesiastical organization at Tây Ninh while other Cao Đài branches formed their own administrations. Colonial rule, anticolonial politics, war, diaspora, and state regulation all shaped its twentieth-century history.

## Distinctives and comparative notes

Cao Đài presents religious history as progressive revelation and places figures from multiple traditions within a universal sacred order. Worship combines Vietnamese ancestor rites, vegetarian observance, meditation, séances, colorful liturgy, and a hierarchical clergy. Calling it merely a mixture misses its distinctive theology and institutional history: borrowed symbols are reorganized into a specifically Vietnamese program of revelation and salvation.
