# Tenrikyo

## Historical context

Tenrikyo dates its founding to Nakayama Miki’s revelatory experience in 1838 at what became Jiba, the tradition’s sacred center in Nara. The community grew through healing, pilgrimage, song, and missionary networks. Meiji regulation compelled institutional association with Sect Shinto, from which Tenrikyo gained legal independence after World War II.

## Distinctives and comparative notes

God the Parent created humanity for the Joyous Life, realized through gratitude, mutual help, inner transformation, and *hinokishin* service. The seated service and sacred dances embody its revealed teaching. Its branch link records historical classification, not a claim that current adherents understand Tenrikyo merely as Shinto; the tradition maintains its own founder, scriptures, cosmology, and global institutions.
