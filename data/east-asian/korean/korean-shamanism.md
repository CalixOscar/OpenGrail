# Korean Shamanic Traditions

## Historical context

Ritual specialists commonly called *mudang* have served Korean households and communities through changing kingdoms, Confucian governance, colonial suppression, war, urbanization, and heritage policy. Regional lineages differ: some specialists inherit roles, while others understand initiation through spirit illness and calling. “Muism” is a modern umbrella rather than a single church.

## Distinctives and comparative notes

The *gut* combines offerings, music, dance, costume, narrative, divination, and spirit embodiment to heal, guide the dead, secure fortune, or restore relationships. Ancestors, generals, Buddhist figures, and local gods may share a ritual world. Calling the tradition only superstition reproduces elite polemic; romanticizing it as unchanged antiquity ignores continuing innovation and professional practice.
