# Ukrainian Native Faith

## Modern formation

Ukrainian Native Faith includes several twentieth- and twenty-first-century movements shaped by émigré, Soviet, post-Soviet, folkloric, and nationalist contexts. Some are polytheist reconstructions; others offer reformulated monotheistic or philosophical systems. Organizations such as RUNVira represent only part of this broader and internally contested field.

## Reconstruction and distinction

Practitioners draw on chronicles, archaeology, folklore, language, and modern religious texts, but no intact pre-Christian Ukrainian liturgy survived for straightforward restoration. The umbrella must not collapse all groups into one theology or political stance. War, diaspora, decolonization, and debates over identity continue to reshape the movement and its public presentation.
