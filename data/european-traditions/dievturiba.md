# Dievturība

## Modern formation

Ernests Brastiņš and collaborators organized Dievturība in interwar Latvia, interpreting the vast Latvian *daina* song corpus as a foundation for renewed indigenous religion. Soviet repression disrupted the movement, while diaspora communities preserved it and post-Soviet Latvia saw revival. Organizations differ in ritual form, theology, and public role.

## Reconstruction and distinction

Dievturi commonly honor Dievs, Māra, Laima, ancestors, seasonal festivals, and Latvian ethical-cultural inheritance. Folklore supplies rich evidence but was collected after centuries of Christianity and does not mechanically reproduce an ancient creed. Dievturība parallels Lithuanian Romuva while remaining a distinct Latvian movement rather than a branch of the same organization.
