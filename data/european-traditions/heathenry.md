# Heathenry

## Historical context

Heathenry, including groups using names such as Ásatrú, emerged as organized modern religion in the later twentieth century, although romantic and folkloric precedents are older. Communities use medieval texts, archaeology, folklore, and comparative reconstruction to build contemporary practice. There is no single international authority, and debates over historical method, inclusivity, politics, and ethnic identity are significant.

## Distinctives and comparative notes

Offerings called *blót*, communal toasts, hospitality, reciprocity, ancestors, and relationships with gods or land powers are common but not universal. The influence edge identifies ancient source material without claiming institutional continuity. Inclusive and anti-racist Heathens explicitly oppose racialist currents that have also appropriated Germanic symbols. Treating the latter as representative of all Heathenry would obscure the field's internal ethical and organizational conflict.
