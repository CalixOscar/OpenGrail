# Forn Siðr

## Modern formation

Names meaning “old custom” became common among Scandinavian pagan organizations during the late twentieth-century revival. Independent bodies in Denmark, Sweden, Norway, and elsewhere developed public rites, life-cycle ceremonies, legal recognition, and local congregational structures. They share sources and broad identity but do not form one international church.

## Reconstruction and distinction

Practice may include *blót*, seasonal gatherings, ancestor remembrance, and devotion to Norse gods and local powers. Modern values and national legal contexts shape these communities as much as medieval texts do. “Forn Siðr” is therefore a family label inside Heathenry, not the historical name of one standardized Viking-age religion or an ethnic test for participation.
