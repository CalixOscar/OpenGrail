# Polish Rodzimowierstwo

## Modern formation

Polish Slavic Native Faith gained visible organizations after the end of communist rule, drawing on earlier romantic, folkloric, and interwar precedents. Communities reconstruct seasonal festivals, offerings, rites of passage, and relations with Slavic gods and ancestors. There is no single hierarchy, scripture, or agreed theology across the movement.

## Reconstruction and distinction

The term *rodzimowierstwo* emphasizes native or ancestral faith, but participants disagree over ethnicity, politics, historical rigor, and openness. Surviving evidence for pre-Christian West Slavic religion is fragmentary and often externally authored. Modern practice therefore combines research and creative religious construction; it should not be projected unchanged into the medieval past.
