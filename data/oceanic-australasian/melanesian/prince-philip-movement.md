# Prince Philip Movement

## Historical context

Some people in Yaohnanen and neighboring communities on Tanna came to identify Britain’s Prince Philip with a powerful overseas figure interpreted through local kastom narratives. Exchanges of photographs and gifts with the royal household made the relationship materially reciprocal. Philip’s death in 2021 prompted mourning and discussion, while community interpretations continued rather than ending on an outsider’s timetable.

## Distinctives and comparative notes

The movement belongs to a particular colonial and kastom history, not to a universal Pacific fascination with European goods. “Cargo cult” remains a familiar scholarly category but can belittle coherent Indigenous political and religious reasoning. Descriptions should follow community accounts and avoid presenting participants as naïve people who literally mistook a celebrity for a generic god.
