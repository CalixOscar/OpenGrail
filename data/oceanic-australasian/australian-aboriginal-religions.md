# Australian Aboriginal Religions

## Historical context

Hundreds of Aboriginal nations maintain distinct languages, Countries, laws, kin systems, and ceremonial responsibilities developed over tens of thousands of years. British invasion brought massacre, dispossession, missionization, stolen children, ecological damage, and restrictions on ceremony. Communities preserved and renewed knowledge under those conditions. “Aboriginal religion” is an external umbrella, never a substitute for a named people and Country.

## Distinctives and comparative notes

English terms such as “Dreaming” or “Dreamtime” translate several non-equivalent concepts relating ancestral action, law, place, story, and ongoing responsibility; they do not describe a remote mythic past alone. Knowledge may be restricted by kin, gender, initiation, or season. Art and songlines are not generic spiritual commodities. Ethical study respects Indigenous data sovereignty, land rights, and community decisions about what can be shown.
