# Tongan Religious Traditions

## Historical context

Tonga’s island polities linked sacred and political rank through the Tuʻi Tonga and other chiefly lines while maintaining wide voyaging networks. Wesleyan, Catholic, and other missions gained influence in the nineteenth century amid civil conflict and state consolidation. Churches now structure much communal life in Tonga and a large diaspora, alongside genealogical and chiefly obligations.

## Distinctives and comparative notes

Kinship, gift exchange, land, rank, funeral responsibilities, dance, and sacred restrictions carry religious force even when not labeled separately from Christianity. Tongan *lotu* refers to worship and religious affiliation but has acquired locally specific meanings. Describing contemporary practice as a layer of Christianity over an untouched ancient religion misses two centuries of active Tongan translation and institution-building.
