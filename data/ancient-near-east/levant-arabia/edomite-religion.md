# Edomite Religion

## Historical setting

Edomite communities south and southeast of the Dead Sea developed political institutions during the Iron Age, with Qos appearing prominently in personal names and later Idumean evidence. Shrines, ceramics, inscriptions, and neighboring accounts suggest a religious world tied to caravan routes, local landscapes, households, and royal authority. Population movement carried Idumean traditions westward in later centuries.

## Scope and interpretation

Qos is securely associated with Edom, but surviving sources do not support a complete mythology or fixed theology. Similarities with neighboring Levantine religions reflect shared language, exchange, and comparable political forms. Attempts to identify Qos wholesale with another regional god remain hypotheses rather than established facts.
