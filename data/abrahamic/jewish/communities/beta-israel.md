# Beta Israel

## Historical context

Beta Israel developed in the Ethiopian highlands, although proposed origin stories range from ancient Israelite descent to gradual local formation; evidence does not support a single certain account. The community maintained priestly leaders called *qesim*, Ge'ez texts, and ritual institutions for centuries. Most members migrated to Israel in the late twentieth century, where recognition, integration, and discrimination became major concerns.

## Distinctives and comparative notes

The Orit, incorporating the Torah and related books, anchors Beta Israel practice alongside distinctive Sabbath, purity, fasting, and pilgrimage traditions. Historically the community did not organize life around the Talmud in the same way as Rabbinic Judaism. Contact with global Jewish institutions and life in Israel have encouraged substantial rabbinization, while qesim and communal activists work to preserve Ethiopian Jewish heritage. Comparisons with Ethiopian Christianity require care and do not erase a distinct Jewish identity.
