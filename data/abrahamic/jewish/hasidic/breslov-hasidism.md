# Breslov Hasidism

## Historical development

Rebbe Nachman, a great-grandson of the Baal Shem Tov, established his circle in Ukraine and spent his final years in Breslov. After his death in 1810 the movement appointed no successor rebbe; Nathan of Nemirov preserved and published Nachman's teachings. Pilgrimage to Nachman's grave in Uman became a major communal practice.

## Teaching and practice

Breslov teaching stresses hope, joy, honest self-examination, simple faith, and *hitbodedut*, spontaneous personal prayer to God. Stories and paradoxes address spiritual struggle as well as mystical ascent. Contemporary Breslov includes established Haredi communities, outreach networks, and diverse new adherents rather than one centralized institution.
