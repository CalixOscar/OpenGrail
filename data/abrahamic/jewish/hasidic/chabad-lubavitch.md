# Chabad-Lubavitch

## Historical development

Shneur Zalman of Liadi founded Chabad in the late eighteenth century within the expanding Hasidic movement. Its name abbreviates the Hebrew faculties of wisdom, understanding, and knowledge. After relocation through Belarus and Russia, the movement's leadership centered in the Lubavitch dynasty and eventually in New York.

## Teaching and practice

Chabad integrates Lurianic Kabbalah with disciplined intellectual contemplation, Hasidic prayer, and halakhic observance. Under Menachem Mendel Schneerson it built a global network of emissaries, schools, synagogues, and public Jewish programs. Messianic interpretations of the late rebbe vary among adherents and remain an internal subject of debate.
