# Lurianic Kabbalah

## Historical development

Isaac Luria taught a circle of kabbalists in Safed during the final years of his life, leaving transmission chiefly to disciples such as Hayyim Vital. Their writings organized accounts of divine contraction, cosmic rupture, exile, and restoration into a powerful interpretive system that spread rapidly through Jewish communities.

## Concepts and influence

Lurianic teaching describes *tzimtzum*, the breaking of vessels, scattered divine sparks, and *tikkun*, or repair, accomplished through commandments and mystical intention. It profoundly shaped prayer, ethics, messianism, and Hasidism. Different schools interpret its symbols literally, metaphysically, or psychologically, so no single summary exhausts the tradition.
