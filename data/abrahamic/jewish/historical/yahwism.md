# Pre-exilic Yahwism

## Evidence and development

“Yahwism” is a modern scholarly term for worship centered on YHWH before the Babylonian exile. Biblical texts and epigraphic evidence reveal regional diversity, devotion to YHWH alongside other divine beings, and repeated efforts by royal or priestly reformers to centralize sacrifice and suppress competing cults.

## Interpretive cautions

The evidence does not describe a single denomination or uniform creed. Scholars debate when exclusive monotheism became dominant and how official ideals related to household religion. This node therefore represents a historical phase within ancient Israelite religion, while later Jewish tradition interprets Israel's early history through its own theological categories.
