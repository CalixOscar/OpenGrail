# Ancient Israelite Religion

## Historical setting

Ancient Israelite religion developed among Iron Age communities in the southern Levant and changed substantially across the monarchic, exilic, and post-exilic periods. Archaeology, inscriptions, and the layered texts of the Hebrew Bible indicate local sanctuaries, sacrifice, household practices, royal cults, and growing concentration on the God of Israel.

## Relationship to Judaism

Scholars distinguish this varied ancient religion from the later institutions and textual traditions called Judaism. Exclusive worship of YHWH, covenantal identity, Torah traditions, and the Jerusalem temple became increasingly central, especially through exile and Persian rule.
