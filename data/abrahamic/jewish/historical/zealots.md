# Zealots

## Historical setting

Josephus uses language of zeal and identifies a specific Zealot faction during the Jewish revolt of 66–70 CE, while later usage sometimes applies the name broadly to anti-Roman militants. Resistance drew on political grievances, temple concerns, apocalyptic hopes, and the conviction that Israel owed ultimate allegiance to God.

## Classification

Historians distinguish the Jerusalem Zealots from the Sicarii and avoid treating every rebel as a member of one party. Nearly all evidence comes from hostile or retrospective sources, especially Josephus. The node records a recognizable current within Second Temple Jewish history while preserving uncertainty about membership, organization, and ideology.
