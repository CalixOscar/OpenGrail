# Amoraic Judaism

## Historical setting

The amoraim were generations of rabbinic interpreters who discussed the Mishnah in Palestine and Sasanian Babylonia from roughly the third through fifth centuries. Their teachings circulated within scholarly networks and were shaped into the Jerusalem and Babylonian Talmuds through extended processes of transmission and editing.

## Enduring importance

Amoraic argument joins legal analysis, biblical interpretation, narrative, ethics, medicine, and folklore. The Babylonian Talmud eventually acquired exceptional authority across much of the Jewish world, while Palestinian traditions remained influential in liturgy and interpretation. “Amoraic Judaism” names a scholarly period and culture rather than a separate modern denomination.
