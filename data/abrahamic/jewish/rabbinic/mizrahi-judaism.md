# Mizrahi Judaism

## Historical development

Jewish communities lived for centuries in Iraq, Iran, Yemen, Syria, Central Asia, the Caucasus, and other Asian and North African settings. The modern Israeli label *Mizrahi*, meaning “Eastern,” grouped populations with distinct languages, rites, and histories during large migrations in the twentieth century.

## Classification and diversity

Mizrahi is not one denomination or a single inherited rite. Some communities adopted Sephardic liturgy and legal authorities, making Sephardi and Mizrahi identities overlap without being synonymous. Babylonian, Persian, Yemenite, Kurdish, Bukharan, and other traditions retain their own histories within the umbrella.
