# Church of Almighty God

## Historical context

The Church of Almighty God (*Quannengshen*), also widely known in China as Eastern
Lightning (*Dongfang Shandian*), was established in 1991 in Henan Province, China.
The movement arose within the rapidly expanding Chinese underground Christian house
church network and was specifically influenced by the Shouters (*Huhanpai*), a
charismatic offshoot of Watchman Nee and Witness Lee's Local Church tradition. The
administrative leader and organizer of the church is Zhao Weishan (born 1951), a
former physics teacher and Shouter leader from Heilongjiang.

In 1991, a woman within the movement, identified by scholars as Yang Xiangbin (born
1973), began uttering first-person prophetic pronouncements in the voice of God.
Zhao and the surrounding believers came to recognize these utterances as the direct
words of the returned Jesus Christ, who had taken human form a second time as a Chinese
woman. In 1995, the Chinese government classified the group as a dangerous cult
(*xie jiao*), initiating extensive crackdowns and arrests. Zhao Weishan and Yang
Xiangbin fled China in 2000, receiving political asylum in the United States in 2001,
from where they continue to direct the international movement. Facing continued
suppression in mainland China, tens of thousands of members have fled abroad,
establishing substantial diaspora communities and production studios in South Korea,
Japan, Italy, Spain, Canada, and the United States.

## Distinctives and comparative notes

The theological core of the Church of Almighty God centers on a tri-dispensational
understanding of human history: the Age of Law in the Old Testament, where God acted
as Yahweh to establish moral commandments; the Age of Grace in the New Testament,
where God incarnated as Jesus Christ to redeem humanity from sin through the
Crucifixion; and the current Age of Kingdom (or Age of Word), begun in 1991, where God
has returned in the flesh as Almighty God to cleanse human nature of its innate
corruption through divine judgment.

The movement rejects traditional Christian Trinitarian orthodoxy, asserting that God
is one single spirit who adopts different names, genders, and physical forms in
different historical dispensations. Members consider the compilation *The Word Appears
in the Flesh* to be the living, primary scripture of the current age, superseding the
historical Bible. The church is famous for its apocalyptic interpretation of the Book
of Revelation, identifying the biblical "Great Red Dragon" with the Chinese Communist
Party (CCP), which it views as the ultimate earthly embodiment of Satan doomed to
destruction. In diaspora, the movement is noted for high-budget digital media
production, generating hundreds of feature films, musical theater broadcasts, and
hymn recordings distributed freely across international online platforms.
