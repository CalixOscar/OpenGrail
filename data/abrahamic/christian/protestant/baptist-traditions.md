# Baptist Traditions

## Historical context

The earliest Baptist congregations appeared among English Separatists in Amsterdam and England in the early seventeenth century. General Baptists held Arminian theology, while Particular Baptists adopted Calvinist positions. Religious liberty advocacy, revival, migration, enslavement and emancipation, missions, and voluntary associations produced numerous Black, white, and global Baptist institutions.

## Distinctives and comparative notes

Baptists normally administer baptism to professing believers by immersion and understand the local congregation as a primary locus of church authority. They defend liberty of conscience and reject state coercion in faith, though historical practice has not always met that ideal. Baptist life ranges from sacramental and ecumenical to revivalist and fundamentalist. Direct descent from continental Anabaptists remains debated; similarities do not establish a single institutional lineage.
