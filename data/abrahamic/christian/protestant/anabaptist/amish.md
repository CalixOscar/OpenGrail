# Amish Traditions

## Historical development

A late seventeenth-century dispute among Swiss and Alsatian Anabaptists separated followers of Jakob Ammann from other Mennonites over discipline, shunning, Communion, and visible conformity. Migration established enduring Amish settlements in North America, while European communities eventually disappeared or joined related churches.

## Community and diversity

Amish church districts regulate technology, dress, transportation, schooling, and social relations through local custom called the *Ordnung*. Adult baptism, mutual aid, nonresistance, and face-to-face community are central. Old Order, New Order, Beachy, and other affiliations differ substantially, so popular images of one uniform Amish culture are misleading.
