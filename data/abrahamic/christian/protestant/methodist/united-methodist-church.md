# United Methodist Church

## Historical development

The United Methodist Church formed in 1968 from the Methodist Church and the Evangelical United Brethren Church, joining Wesleyan, German Pietist, and American denominational histories. Conferences in Africa, Europe, the Philippines, and the United States share connectional institutions under bishops and a quadrennial General Conference.

## Theology and recent division

United Methodism combines Wesleyan teaching on grace and sanctification with liturgy, evangelism, education, and social action. It baptizes infants and adults and welcomes Christians to Communion. Prolonged conflict over LGBTQ inclusion led many congregations to disaffiliate and contributed to formation of the Global Methodist Church, leaving a changing worldwide communion.
