# United Pentecostal Church International

## Historical development

The United Pentecostal Church International formed in 1945 through the merger of the Pentecostal Church, Incorporated and the Pentecostal Assemblies of Jesus Christ. Based initially in North America, it developed a large international missionary and congregational network. Its history reflects both cooperation and racial division within twentieth-century Pentecostalism.

## Belief and practice

The UPCI teaches Oneness theology, baptism in the name of Jesus Christ, and Spirit baptism associated with speaking in tongues. Congregations practice energetic worship, evangelism, divine healing, and holiness disciplines. Local churches have pastoral leadership within district and general structures, and the denomination distinguishes its theology from Trinitarian Pentecostal bodies such as the Assemblies of God.
