# Pentecostal Assemblies of the World

## Historical development

The Pentecostal Assemblies of the World traces its beginnings to early Pentecostal cooperation during the Azusa Street revival era. It became associated with the Oneness position after 1913 and, under leaders including Garfield T. Haywood, developed an especially important African American membership and ministry. Segregation-era divisions and later reunifications shaped its institutional history.

## Belief and practice

The denomination teaches one God revealed in Jesus Christ, baptism in Jesus' name, and reception of the Holy Spirit with speaking in tongues. Worship emphasizes preaching, music, healing, testimony, and moral holiness. Bishops provide episcopal oversight within diocesan and international structures, distinguishing its governance from more congregational Pentecostal bodies.
