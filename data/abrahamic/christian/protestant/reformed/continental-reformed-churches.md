# Continental Reformed Churches

## Historical development

Reformed churches emerged through Swiss reformers including Zwingli, Bullinger, and Calvin and developed distinct confessional traditions in the Netherlands, German territories, France, Hungary, and elsewhere. Persecution, state establishments, migration, and colonial expansion carried these churches beyond Europe.

## Theology and diversity

Continental Reformed identity often draws on the Heidelberg Catechism, Belgic Confession, and Canons of Dort, while regional churches use additional standards. Covenant theology, preaching, disciplined worship, and presbyterial-synodal governance are recurrent themes. The family includes Dutch Reformed, Swiss Reformed, Hungarian Reformed, French Huguenot, and many independent successor churches.
