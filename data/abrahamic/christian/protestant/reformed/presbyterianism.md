# Presbyterianism

## Historical development

Presbyterianism developed through the Scottish Reformation, especially the work of John Knox and the Reformed settlement of 1560. Churches carried presbyterian polity and confessional theology to Ireland, North America, Africa, Asia, and Oceania. Revivals, migration, doctrinal controversies, and mergers produced many national denominations.

## Theology and governance

Presbyterian churches are governed by connected councils of teaching and ruling elders rather than bishops or wholly independent congregations. Reformed emphases include divine sovereignty, covenant, preaching, sacraments, and disciplined communal life. Contemporary bodies range from confessionally conservative to theologically liberal and cannot be represented by one national church.
