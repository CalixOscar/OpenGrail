# Lutheran Church—Missouri Synod

## Historical development

German Lutheran pastors and congregations organized the Missouri Synod in Chicago in 1847 under the leadership of C. F. W. Walther and others. The denomination developed parochial schools, seminaries, publishing, and global missions while maintaining a strong confessional identity. Twentieth-century theological disputes produced both departures and internal realignments.

## Theology and practice

The LCMS subscribes to the Book of Concord as a faithful exposition of scripture, teaches biblical inerrancy, and practices closed or close Communion. It ordains men to the pastoral office while recognizing other service roles for women. Congregations possess local legal autonomy within a synodical system of doctrine, districts, and elected leadership.
