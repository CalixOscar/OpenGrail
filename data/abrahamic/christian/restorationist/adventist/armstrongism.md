# Armstrongist Churches of God

## Historical development

Herbert W. Armstrong began a radio ministry after association with the Oregon Conference of the Church of God (Seventh Day) and organized the Radio Church of God in 1934. Renamed the Worldwide Church of God, it built an international media and educational network. After Armstrong's death, doctrinal reforms produced both an evangelical successor church and numerous bodies committed to aspects of his earlier teaching.

## Belief and classification

Historic Armstrongism combined seventh-day Sabbath and biblical festivals with non-Trinitarian theology, conditional immortality, a distinctive account of God's future family, and British Israelism. Descendant churches retain these features in different combinations. “Armstrongism” is an analytical family label, not the official name of a single contemporary denomination.
