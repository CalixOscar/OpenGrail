# Church of God General Conference

## Historical development

The Church of God General Conference, sometimes identified historically with the “Abrahamic Faith,” grew from nineteenth-century Adventist and restorationist congregations associated with Benjamin Wilson and related preachers. Regional bodies cooperated for decades before forming a general conference in 1921. Its history overlaps with but remains institutionally distinct from both Advent Christian and Christadelphian traditions.

## Belief and practice

The denomination teaches the unity of God, Jesus as the human Messiah and Son of God, conditional immortality, resurrection, and the future kingdom of God on earth. It rejects classical Trinitarian formulation and eternal conscious torment. Congregations are locally governed and recognize the Bible rather than a later prophetic corpus as their authority.
