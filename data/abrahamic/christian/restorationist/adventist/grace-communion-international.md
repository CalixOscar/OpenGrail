# Grace Communion International

## Historical development

After Herbert W. Armstrong's death, leaders of the Worldwide Church of God gradually rejected British Israelism, required festival observance, non-Trinitarian teaching, and other inherited doctrines. These changes accelerated publicly in 1995 and prompted substantial membership loss and many new denominations. The remaining church adopted the name Grace Communion International in 2009.

## Belief and identity

Grace Communion International now affirms the Trinity, salvation by grace, and mainstream evangelical Christian doctrine, and participates in evangelical associations. It does not require Sabbath or festival observance. Its placement under the Armstrongist family records institutional descent, not present agreement with Armstrong's theology, much of which the denomination has explicitly repudiated.
