# Advent Christian Church

## Historical development

The Advent Christian Church emerged when several post-Millerite associations joined in the Advent Christian Association in 1860. George Storrs and other preachers helped spread conditionalist interpretations of death and resurrection within the wider Adventist environment. The denomination developed congregations, conferences, missions, and educational institutions while remaining distinct from Seventh-day Adventism.

## Belief and practice

Advent Christians emphasize the visible return of Christ, resurrection at his coming, and conditional immortality rather than an inherently immortal soul. They normally worship on Sunday and do not recognize Ellen G. White as a prophetic authority. Congregational freedom is balanced by regional and general conferences, and the Bible is treated as the primary rule of faith.
