# Philadelphia Church of God

## Historical development

Gerald Flurry founded the Philadelphia Church of God in 1989 after his dismissal from the Worldwide Church of God. The new body opposed doctrinal changes under Joseph W. Tkach and presented itself as preserving Herbert W. Armstrong's teachings. It developed the *Philadelphia Trumpet*, broadcasting ministries, and educational institutions based in Oklahoma.

## Belief and practice

The church observes the seventh-day Sabbath and biblical festivals and retains non-Trinitarian, apocalyptic, and British-Israelite elements of historic Armstrongism. It also accords a distinctive prophetic role to Flurry's *Malachi's Message*. The church is separate from other Armstrongist successors and should not be treated as representative of all Churches of God.
