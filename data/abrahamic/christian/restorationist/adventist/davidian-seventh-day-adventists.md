# Davidian Seventh-day Adventists

## Historical development

Victor Houteff began promoting *The Shepherd's Rod* among Seventh-day Adventists in 1929, presenting new interpretations of prophecy and a purified end-time remnant. After his exclusion from the denomination, followers established a community near Waco, Texas, called Mount Carmel Center. Houteff's death in 1955 led to succession disputes and several Davidian organizations.

## Belief and classification

Davidians retain major Seventh-day Adventist teachings, the authority of Ellen White, and additional interpretations attributed to Houteff. They expect an end-time purification and restoration connected with biblical Israel. The broader Davidian tradition is not identical with the Branch Davidians, one later offshoot whose history and leadership developed separately.
