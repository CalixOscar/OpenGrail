# Branch Davidians

## Historical development

The Branch Davidian line emerged after Victor Houteff's death when Benjamin Roden proclaimed a new “Branch” message within the Davidian movement. Leadership later passed through Lois Roden and competing claimants. David Koresh gained control of the Mount Carmel community, which ended in the deadly 1993 federal siege; other Branch Davidian believers and successor groups continued independently.

## Belief and classification

Branch Davidian theology combines Adventist and Davidian apocalyptic interpretation with claims of continuing prophetic revelation. Beliefs varied significantly by leader and period, and Koresh's community should not stand for all historical Davidians or Seventh-day Adventists. The graph therefore places this body two lineage levels below Seventh-day Adventism and does not imply responsibility by its parent traditions.
