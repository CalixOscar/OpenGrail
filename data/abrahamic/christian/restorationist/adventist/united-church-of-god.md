# United Church of God, an International Association

## Historical development

The United Church of God was organized in 1995 by ministers and members departing the Worldwide Church of God as that body adopted evangelical doctrines. It created a council-led governance structure intended to avoid concentrating authority in one individual. Congregations and media ministries now operate across many countries.

## Belief and practice

The church observes the seventh-day Sabbath and biblical annual festivals, teaches conditional immortality, and anticipates Christ's millennial reign. It rejects the Trinity and understands God as a family into which redeemed humans may ultimately be born. It retains much of Herbert Armstrong's religious framework while differing from other successor churches in governance and some interpretations.
