# International Christian Church

## Historical development

The International Christian Church coalesced in Portland, Oregon, around Kip McKean and congregations that separated from the International Churches of Christ during the mid-2000s. Its leaders presented the new network as a renewed continuation of the earlier discipling and mission model. Church planting campaigns subsequently established congregations in multiple countries.

## Organization and practice

The movement teaches baptism by immersion for disciples, structured personal discipling, and an obligation to evangelize the world. It maintains a more coordinated global mission program than the decentralized post-2003 International Churches of Christ. The two bodies share close Stone-Campbell ancestry but are institutionally separate and differ over leadership, governance, and the interpretation of their common history.
