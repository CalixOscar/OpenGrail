# Christian Church (Disciples of Christ)

## Historical development

The Disciples of Christ descend from the Stone and Campbell reform movements that joined in the 1830s. Cooperative missionary, educational, and benevolent institutions developed alongside congregational independence. A long twentieth-century restructure culminated in the 1968 adoption of *The Design*, giving the Christian Church (Disciples of Christ) a recognizable general, regional, and congregational organization.

## Organization and practice

Disciples congregations center worship on the Lord's Supper and usually practice baptism by immersion while recognizing Christians from other baptismal traditions. The denomination participates extensively in ecumenical work and permits substantial theological diversity. It shares Stone-Campbell ancestry with Churches of Christ and independent Christian Churches but differs from both in institutional organization and its generally mainline Protestant orientation.
