# Remnant Church of Jesus Christ of Latter Day Saints

## Historical development

The Remnant Church was organized in 2000 by participants in the Restoration Branch movement who sought a unified church and renewed lineal prophetic presidency. It established headquarters in Independence, Missouri, and gathered congregations that shared a conservative Reorganization heritage. Other Restoration Branches remained independent and did not accept the new body's authority claims.

## Belief and organization

The church affirms the Bible, Book of Mormon, and an open Doctrine and Covenants, together with sacraments and priesthood forms inherited from the Reorganized tradition. It teaches the gathering and building of Zion and is governed through a presidency, apostles, bishops, and other priesthood offices. Its branch relationship here records institutional genealogy rather than acceptance of its theological succession claim.
