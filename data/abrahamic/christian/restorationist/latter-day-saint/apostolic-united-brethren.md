# Apostolic United Brethren

## Historical development

The Apostolic United Brethren emerged from a 1954 division among Latter Day Saint fundamentalists associated with the priesthood council and Short Creek community. Followers of Joseph White Musser's succession line developed a body separate from the organization that became the FLDS Church. Its members have been concentrated in the Intermountain West while also forming communities elsewhere.

## Belief and organization

The AUB accepts the standard works of the Latter Day Saint tradition and maintains plural marriage as a religious principle. It operates through a priesthood council and affiliated congregations while lacking recognition from the Utah-based church. Although grouped under Latter Day Saint fundamentalism, its leadership, institutional culture, and historical trajectory are distinct from those of the FLDS Church.
