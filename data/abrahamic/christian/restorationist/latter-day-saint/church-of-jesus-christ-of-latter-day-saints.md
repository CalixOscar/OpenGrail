# The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints

## Historical development

After Joseph Smith's death in 1844, most Latter Day Saints who accepted the leadership of the Quorum of the Twelve Apostles followed Brigham Young to the Great Basin. The Utah-based church developed a worldwide missionary system, temples, lay congregational leadership, and extensive educational and welfare institutions. It discontinued authorization of new plural marriages around the turn of the twentieth century and today is by far the movement's largest body.

## Belief and practice

Members affirm continuing revelation, restored priesthood authority, baptism and confirmation, temple covenants, eternal families, and the potential for human exaltation through Jesus Christ. Alongside the Bible, the church recognizes the Book of Mormon, Doctrine and Covenants, and Pearl of Great Price as scripture. “Mormon” remains historically familiar, but the church asks that its full name or “the Church of Jesus Christ” be used in most current references.
