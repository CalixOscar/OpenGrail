# Church of Christ (Temple Lot)

## Historical development

The Church of Christ (Temple Lot) descends from Latter Day Saints who did not join the largest post-succession bodies. Granville Hedrick helped organize the church in Illinois in 1863, and members later returned to Independence, Missouri. The church owns the portion of land identified in early movement revelations as the site for a future temple.

## Belief and practice

Temple Lot members accept the Bible and Book of Mormon and use the earlier Book of Commandments, while rejecting plural marriage, baptism for the dead, and several later theological developments. The church is governed through apostles and local ministries rather than a single prophetic president. Its stewardship of the Temple Lot gives it significance beyond its relatively small membership.
