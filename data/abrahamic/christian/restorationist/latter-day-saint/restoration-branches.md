# Restoration Branches

## Historical development

Restoration Branches formed chiefly during the 1980s as conservative members and congregations opposed changes in the Reorganized Church of Jesus Christ of Latter Day Saints, particularly women's ordination and evolving theology. Rather than unite in one denomination, many organized as locally governed branches linked by conferences, ministries, and informal associations.

## Belief and organization

Branches generally preserve older Reorganization scriptures, priesthood offices, sacramental practice, and an expectation of Zion centered in Independence, Missouri. They reject plural marriage and distinguish their tradition from Utah-based Latter-day Saint bodies. Because there is no universally governing organization, beliefs and cooperative relationships vary among congregations.
