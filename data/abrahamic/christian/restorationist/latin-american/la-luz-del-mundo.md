# La Luz del Mundo

## Historical development

Eusebio Joaquín González, known in the movement as Aarón, founded La Luz del Mundo in Guadalajara in 1926. The church grew through missionary activity, strong community institutions, distinctive temples, and leadership successions within the Joaquín family. It now has congregations across Mexico and in international migrant communities.

## Belief and practice

The church teaches restoration of the original Christian church through a living apostle, baptism in the name of Jesus Christ, disciplined congregational life, and non-Trinitarian theology. Annual Holy Supper gatherings are central to communal identity. Its official Spanish name translates as “Church of the Living God, Pillar and Ground of the Truth, The Light of the World.”
