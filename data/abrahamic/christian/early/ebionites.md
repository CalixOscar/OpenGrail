# Ebionites

## Historical evidence

Christian heresiologists described Ebionites as Torah-observant followers of Jesus who rejected Paul, used one or more Jewish-Christian gospels, and denied Christ's preexistent divinity. Accounts differ across Irenaeus, Origen, Epiphanius, and other writers and span several centuries, making it uncertain whether they describe one continuous organization.

## Classification

“Ebionite” may combine distinct Jewish-Christian communities under an outsider label derived from the Hebrew word for poor. Their views of virgin birth, sacrifice, and Christology cannot be reconstructed with complete confidence. The node preserves their importance to early Christian diversity while avoiding a simple identification with the Jerusalem community of Jesus's first followers.
