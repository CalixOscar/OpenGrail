# New Prophecy Movement (Montanism)

## Historical development

Montanus and the women prophets Priscilla and Maximilla began ecstatic prophecy in second-century Phrygia, announcing the Spirit's urgent guidance and an approaching eschatological fulfillment associated with Pepuza. The movement spread to other regions and attracted Tertullian in North Africa before many bishops rejected its prophets.

## Practice and interpretation

The New Prophecy promoted fasting, moral rigor, martyrdom, and continued charismatic revelation while using broadly Christian scripture. Ancient critics charged its prophets with excess and false inspiration, but surviving evidence is largely polemical. Modern scholarship treats it as a diverse Christian renewal movement rather than reducing it to a stereotype of irrational enthusiasm.
