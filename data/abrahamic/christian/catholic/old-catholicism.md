# Old Catholicism

## Historical context

Old Catholic communities include the eighteenth-century Church of Utrecht and churches organized after the First Vatican Council defined papal infallibility and universal jurisdiction in 1870. These bodies formed the Union of Utrecht in 1889. They later entered full communion with Anglican churches and developed nationally distinct patterns of reform and ecumenical engagement.

## Distinctives and comparative notes

Old Catholics retain episcopal succession, creeds, liturgical worship, and sacraments while rejecting the later Roman claims that precipitated separation. Many member churches ordain women, permit clergy to marry, and bless same-sex unions, though practices are not completely uniform. The label does not include every independent group using “Old Catholic.” Their branch relation records historical origin; it does not imply current jurisdiction under Rome.
