# Coptic Catholic Church

## Historical formation

Catholic missions and changing communities in Ottoman Egypt led Rome to establish an apostolic vicariate for Coptic Catholics in 1741. A Catholic patriarchate of Alexandria was restored on a stable basis in the late nineteenth century. The church remains much smaller than the Coptic Orthodox Church but maintains dioceses, parishes, schools, and religious institutes in Egypt and the diaspora.

## Identity and practice

The Coptic Catholic Church is a patriarchal *sui iuris* church that celebrates the Alexandrian Coptic rite while accepting communion with the bishop of Rome. Its Coptic language, calendar, spirituality, and liturgical forms reflect Egypt’s ancient Christian heritage. Sharing a rite with Coptic Orthodoxy does not erase their separate hierarchies or the doctrinal and jurisdictional history behind that separation.
