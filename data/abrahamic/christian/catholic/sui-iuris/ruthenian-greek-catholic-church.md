# Ruthenian Greek Catholic Church

## Historical formation

The Union of Uzhhorod in 1646 brought a group of Orthodox priests in the Habsburg lands into communion with Rome while preserving Byzantine worship and discipline. Later borders, national movements, suppression under communist governments, and migration complicated Ruthenian institutional identity. In the United States, a metropolitan Byzantine Catholic church developed from Carpatho-Rusyn immigrant communities.

## Identity and practice

The Ruthenian church uses the Byzantine rite and belongs to the family of Eastern Catholic *sui iuris* churches. “Ruthenian” is a historical ecclesial and regional label connected particularly with Carpathian Rusyn heritage; it does not encompass every Slavic Greek Catholic. Closely related liturgical customs do not make it identical to the Ukrainian, Slovak, Hungarian, or Romanian Greek Catholic churches.
