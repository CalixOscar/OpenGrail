# Orthodox Bahá'í Faith

## Historical development

After Shoghi Effendi died in 1957 without appointing a successor Guardian, the Hands of the Cause guided the Bahá'í community toward election of the Universal House of Justice. In 1960 Mason Remey claimed that an earlier appointment made him the second Guardian. Nearly all Bahá'ís rejected the claim; a small following formed separate organizations.

## Authority and classification

Orthodox Bahá'ís accept the central Bahá'í scriptures but argue that hereditary Guardianship must continue alongside other institutions. Mainstream Bahá'í institutions regard Remey's claim as a violation of the Covenant and do not recognize the movement as Bahá'í. This graph edge records historical derivation without adjudicating either community's theological legitimacy.
