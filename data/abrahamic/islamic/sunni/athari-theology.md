# Athari Theology

## Historical development

Early Sunni traditionalists defended doctrine grounded in Quran, hadith, and the statements of the first generations, often resisting the methods of *kalam*, or speculative theology. Ahmad ibn Hanbal became an emblematic authority, though Athari orientation was never limited to jurists of the Hanbali legal school.

## Method and diversity

Athari theologians affirm divine attributes found in revelation while rejecting assimilation of God to creation and frequently avoiding figurative reinterpretation. Later figures such as Ibn Taymiyya reformulated traditionalist arguments in sophisticated ways. Modern Salafi theology draws heavily on this inheritance, but Atharism is historically broader than any one contemporary movement.
