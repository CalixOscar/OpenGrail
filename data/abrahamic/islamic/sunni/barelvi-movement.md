# Barelvi Movement

## Historical development

The movement formed around the scholarship of Ahmad Raza Khan of Bareilly in late nineteenth-century British India. Its scholars defended established Sunni devotional and Sufi practices in polemics with Deobandi, Ahl-i Hadith, and other reform currents. Mosques, seminaries, shrine networks, and political organizations carried the tradition worldwide.

## Teaching and identity

Barelvi scholars are usually Hanafi and affirm intercession, celebration of the Prophet's birth, devotional poetry, saintly charisma, and visitation of shrines within defined theological limits. Adherents often prefer the name *Ahl-e Sunnat wa Jama'at*. The movement contains substantial regional and institutional diversity and is not a single centralized denomination.
