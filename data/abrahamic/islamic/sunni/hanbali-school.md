# Hanbali School

## Historical context

Hanbali jurisprudence formed around Ahmad ibn Hanbal, a major hadith scholar remembered for resisting the Abbasid inquisition over the createdness of the Qur'an. His students collected responses and later jurists systematized a school influential in Baghdad, Syria, Najd, and modern Saudi Arabia. Thinkers such as Ibn Taymiyya were important but do not exhaust Hanbali history.

## Distinctives and comparative notes

The school prioritizes Qur'an, hadith, companion reports, and early precedent while using analogy under constrained conditions. Hanbalis have included quietist jurists, Sufis, reformers, and political actors. Salafism overlaps with portions of Hanbali inheritance but is not synonymous with the legal school; Salafis may decline consistent adherence to any one *madhhab*. Other Sunni schools recognize Hanbali law within classical jurisprudential pluralism.
