# Maturidi Theology

## Historical context

Maturidi theology crystallized around Abu Mansur al-Maturidi of Samarqand and earlier Hanafi theological traditions in Central Asia. Later scholars systematized and spread it through Seljuk, Ottoman, South Asian, and other learned networks. It became one of the two most widespread traditions of Sunni *kalam* alongside Ash'arism.

## Distinctives and comparative notes

Maturidis use rational argument in defense of revelation and generally hold that reason can know basic truths about God and moral accountability. They affirm divine attributes while resisting crude anthropomorphism and develop nuanced accounts of human action under divine creation. Differences from Ash'ari theology are real but usually treated as internal Sunni debates. Association with Hanafi law is historically strong, yet jurisprudence and creed remain analytically distinct disciplines.
