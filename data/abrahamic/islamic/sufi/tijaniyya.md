# Tijaniyya

## Historical development

Ahmad al-Tijani established his path in the late eighteenth-century Maghreb and taught a distinctive direct spiritual authorization from the Prophet Muhammad. Scholars, merchants, and reformers carried the order across the Sahara, where major West African lineages developed around figures such as al-Hajj Umar Tall and Ibrahim Niasse.

## Practice and diversity

Initiates recite prescribed litanies including the *wazifa* and affirm an exclusive commitment to the Tijani path. Communities combine Sufi formation with scholarship, trade, social welfare, and sometimes political leadership. Different branches interpret authority, succession, and the universal reach of spiritual training in their own ways.
