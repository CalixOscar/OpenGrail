# Qadiriyya

## Historical development

The Qadiriyya traces spiritual descent to Abd al-Qadir al-Jilani, a twelfth-century Hanbali preacher in Baghdad whose descendants and admirers spread his reputation. Rather than one centralized institution, Qadiri lineages expanded through North and West Africa, the Middle East, South Asia, and Southeast Asia.

## Practice and diversity

Qadiri practice commonly includes remembrance of God, litanies, ethical discipline, spiritual companionship, and reverence for al-Jilani. Ritual forms and doctrinal emphases differ by region and lineage. Its broad diffusion means “Qadiriyya” identifies a family of initiatic chains rather than a uniform global organization.
