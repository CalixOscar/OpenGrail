# Dawoodi Bohra

## Historical development

A disputed succession to the Tayyibi office of *da'i al-mutlaq* in 1592 separated followers of Dawud ibn Qutb Shah from the Sulaymani line. Dawoodi Bohra communities developed especially in Gujarat and later across the Indian Ocean and global diaspora. A centralized clerical administration coordinates worship, education, charity, and community institutions.

## Religious life

Dawoodi Bohras combine Ismaili theology and esoteric interpretation with distinctive Fatimid-derived ritual, dress, and communal organization. They recognize a concealed imam represented by the living *da'i*. Arabic, Gujarati, and the community language Lisan al-Dawat all shape religious learning and everyday identity.
