# Usuli Twelver Shiism

## Historical development

Usuli jurists developed systematic principles for evaluating traditions and deriving legal judgments, in recurring debate with Akhbari traditionalists. Scholars such as Muhammad Baqir Bihbahani helped establish Usuli predominance in the eighteenth century. Networks of seminaries and senior jurists subsequently became central to Twelver communal authority.

## Authority and practice

Usulism distinguishes qualified legal reasoning, *ijtihad*, from emulation, *taqlid*, by non-specialists who follow a recognized jurist. The office and public role of jurists developed differently across Iran, Iraq, Lebanon, and other regions. Political doctrines such as guardianship of the jurist are particular Usuli interpretations rather than a belief uniformly accepted by all Twelvers.
