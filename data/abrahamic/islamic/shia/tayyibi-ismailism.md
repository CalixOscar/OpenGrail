# Tayyibi Ismailism

## Historical development

Tayyibis recognized the infant al-Tayyib as imam after the death of the Fatimid caliph al-Amir and held that he entered concealment. Queen Arwa al-Sulayhi supported a Yemeni missionary hierarchy led by the *da'i al-mutlaq*, who guides the community on behalf of the hidden imam. The tradition later established a durable center in western India.

## Community structure

Tayyibi thought preserves Fatimid-Ismaili esoteric interpretation, ritual life, and a ranked missionary organization. Disputes over succession to the office of *da'i* produced Dawoodi, Sulaymani, Alavi, and other Bohra communities. They share a religious genealogy while maintaining separate leaders and institutions.
