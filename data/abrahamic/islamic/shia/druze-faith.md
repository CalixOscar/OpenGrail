# Druze Faith

## Historical context

The Druze call began in the early eleventh-century Fatimid realm and was declared closed to new adherents within a few decades. Communities endured in the mountains of present-day Lebanon, Syria, Israel, and Jordan, developing strong local institutions and histories of both autonomy and participation in surrounding states. Druze identity is transmitted by birth rather than conversion.

## Distinctives and comparative notes

The *Epistles of Wisdom* present an esoteric monotheism shaped by Ismaili interpretation and philosophical traditions. The community distinguishes initiated *uqqal*, who study religious texts and follow special disciplines, from uninitiated *juhhal*. Belief in transmigration is prominent. Although the faith emerged historically from Ismailism, Druze generally understand themselves as a distinct community, and the graph's branch relation describes genealogy rather than present denominational membership.
