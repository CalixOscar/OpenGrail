# Cult of Serapis

## Historical setting

Serapis became prominent under the early Ptolemies, combining Egyptian associations of Osiris-Apis with a Greek anthropomorphic image and attributes linked to sovereignty, fertility, healing, and the underworld. Alexandria’s Serapeum was the most famous sanctuary, while the cult spread through ports and cities of the Hellenistic and Roman Mediterranean.

## Scope and interpretation

The cult is a documented institutional synthesis, but it was not an abstract merger of “Greek religion” and “Egyptian religion” in equal measure everywhere. Egyptian and Greek worshipers could interpret Serapis through different ritual and linguistic frames. His frequent association with Isis formed a divine household and transregional cult network rather than a single mandatory theology.
