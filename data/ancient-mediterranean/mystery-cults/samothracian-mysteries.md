# Samothracian Mysteries

## Historical setting

The sanctuary of the Great Gods on Samothrace drew initiates from many regions, including sailors, rulers, and Roman elites. Monumental buildings and dedications expanded especially during the Hellenistic age. Unlike Eleusis, initiation was not restricted by Athenian civic structures, and protection at sea became one celebrated benefit.

## Scope and interpretation

Secrecy obscures ritual detail and even the stable identities of the “Great Gods.” Ancient writers compared them with Cabeiri and other divine groups, but local names and functions should not be harmonized too quickly. The mysteries formed a distinctive sanctuary institution, not a separate doctrine replacing participants’ civic or household worship.
