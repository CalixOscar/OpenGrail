# Cult of Cybele and Attis

## Historical setting

Cult of the Anatolian Mother of the Gods spread through Greek cities and was formally received at Rome as Magna Mater in 204 BCE. Mythic and ritual traditions concerning Attis developed unevenly, especially in imperial times. Sanctuaries, festivals, priestly personnel, dedications, music, and processions reveal both public civic patronage and more specialized devotion.

## Scope and interpretation

Cybele, Meter, and Magna Mater are connected historical forms rather than evidence of one timeless universal mother goddess. The self-castrated *galli* were highly visible specialists but did not represent every participant. Claims that the cult offered a uniform dying-and-rising salvation doctrine simplify fragmentary, locally varied evidence about Attis and initiation.
