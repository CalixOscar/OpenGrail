# Middle Platonism

## Historical setting

From roughly the first century BCE to the third century CE, Platonist thinkers reread Plato alongside Aristotle, Pythagorean traditions, and Stoic debates. Figures such as Plutarch, Alcinous, Apuleius, and Numenius differed considerably. The label “Middle Platonism” was created by modern scholarship and should not imply a unified ancient institution.

## Religious and philosophical scope

These writers explored divine transcendence, intermediary beings, providence, soul, cosmology, and the philosophical interpretation of traditional cult. Their synthesis prepared important problems and vocabulary for Plotinus and later Neoplatonism, but no clean boundary separates every phase. The node maps a learned tradition with strong theological content rather than a popular religion shared by all ancient Platonists.
