# Sasanian Zoroastrianism

## Historical setting

Sasanian rulers patronized fire temples and priestly institutions while presenting kingship as protected by the gods. Inscriptions of Kerdir document clerical power and competition, but state, court, and local religious life were never perfectly uniform. Avestan transmission and Middle Persian exegesis flourished across this period and after the Islamic conquest.

## Scope and interpretation

Many detailed Zoroastrian cosmological and legal works survive in later manuscripts, so dating particular teachings requires care. “State church” can overstate centralized control across four centuries and a diverse empire. Sasanian tradition was formative for later communities, but it should not be projected unchanged backward onto Zarathustra or Achaemenid Iran.
