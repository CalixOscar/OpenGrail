# Khurramism

## Historical setting

Medieval Muslim authors used labels related to *Khurramiyya* for several Iranian movements, most famously the rebellion led by Babak Khorramdin against the Abbasids in the ninth century. Reports attribute distinctive ritual, communal, and reincarnation beliefs, but nearly all detailed descriptions come from opponents. Regional movements grouped under the name may not have shared one organization or theology.

## Scope and interpretation

Scholars often connect Khurramite movements with Mazdakite legacies and older Iranian religious patterns, yet the evidence does not establish simple institutional descent. The branch link therefore carries minority-scholarly certainty. Political revolt, local identity, Islamic-era vocabulary, and inherited cosmologies all likely contributed to a phenomenon reconstructed through highly polemical sources.
