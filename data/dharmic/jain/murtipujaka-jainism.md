# Mūrtipūjaka Śvetāmbara Jainism

## Historical context

Mūrtipūjaka communities constitute the principal image-worshipping stream of Śvetāmbara Jainism. Mendicant *gacchas*, merchant patronage, pilgrimage networks, and ornate temples shaped its medieval and modern history in western India and the diaspora.

## Distinctives and comparative notes

Ritual centers on consecrated images of the Jinas, accompanied by offerings, hymns, meditation, confession, and festival observance. The images are perfected teachers rather than creator gods. Sthānakavāsī and Terāpanth reform currents reject image worship, making ritual form a central internal distinction within Śvetāmbara life.
