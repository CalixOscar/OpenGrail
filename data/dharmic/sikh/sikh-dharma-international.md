# Sikh Dharma International / 3HO

## Historical context

Harbhajan Singh, known as Yogi Bhajan, organized 3HO in North America from the late 1960s. Many converts adopted Sikh names, Khalsa discipline, gurdwara worship, and distinctive white clothing while teaching a modern system marketed as Kundalini Yoga. The movement built religious, educational, and commercial institutions.

## Distinctives and comparative notes

Members combine Sikh scripture and identity with yogic exercises and teachings not generally practiced in Punjabi Sikh communities. Its place within global Sikhism is contested, and investigations after the founder's death documented serious allegations of abuse. Describing the movement requires separating Sikh tradition, its modern yoga system, and institutional conduct.
