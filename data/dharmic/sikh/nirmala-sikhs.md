# Nirmalā Sikhs

## Historical context

Nirmalā tradition links its origins to Guru Gobind Singh's patronage of learned Sikhs, although exact foundation narratives are debated. Nirmalā scholars became prominent interpreters, teachers, and custodians in the eighteenth and nineteenth centuries, often mastering Sanskrit philosophical literature.

## Distinctives and comparative notes

The order interprets Sikh scripture through a wide Indic scholastic vocabulary and historically cultivated ascetic life. Its saffron dress and Vedāntic language distinguish it from dominant Khalsa presentation without erasing devotion to the Gurus and Guru Granth Sahib.
