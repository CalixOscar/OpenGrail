# Nirankari Movement

## Historical context

Baba Dyal founded the original Nirankari movement in nineteenth-century Punjab, calling Sikhs back to devotion to the *nirankar*, the formless divine, and criticizing accretions he regarded as contrary to Guru Nanak’s teaching. The lineage contributed to the wider Sikh reform environment before Singh Sabha institutions became dominant.

## Distinctives and comparative notes

Early Nirankaris favored simple rites, recitation, and rejection of image worship. They should be distinguished from the twentieth-century Sant Nirankari Mission, which developed separately and has had conflictual relations with orthodox Sikh institutions. This node denotes the original Sikh reform lineage; using the shared name without historical qualification can conflate communities with different doctrines and authorities.
