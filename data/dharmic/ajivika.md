# Ājīvika Tradition

## Historical context

Ājīvikas were prominent rivals of Buddhists and Jains in ancient India and received Mauryan patronage, including cave donations. They remained visible for centuries, especially in southern India, before disappearing as an identifiable community. No uncontested Ājīvika scripture survives.

## Distinctives and comparative notes

Rival sources attribute to Ājīvikas *niyati*, an exact cosmic destiny through which beings pass regardless of volitional effort, alongside rigorous asceticism. Because nearly all descriptions come from opponents, details of doctrine and practice remain uncertain. The minority tier reflects source limitations, not doubt that the movement existed.
