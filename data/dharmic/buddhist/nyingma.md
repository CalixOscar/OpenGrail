# Nyingma

## Historical context

Nyingma lineages trace key translations and practices to the eighth- and ninth-century imperial period, especially figures such as Padmasambhava, Śāntarakṣita, and Vimalamitra. Later “treasure” revelation traditions continually renewed the corpus. Unlike schools organized around a single later transmission, Nyingma historically operated through dispersed monasteries, households, and ritual lineages.

## Distinctives and comparative notes

The school classifies teachings in nine vehicles and gives special prominence to Dzogchen, or Great Perfection, while also preserving elaborate tantric ritual and monastic study. *Terma* revelations are understood internally as concealed teachings discovered at appropriate times. Historical study can trace revelation communities without deciding theological claims about concealment or prophetic authorization.
