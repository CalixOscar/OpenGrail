# Dhammayut Nikāya

## Historical context

Prince Mongkut, then a monk and later King Rama IV, organized the Thammayut or Dhammayut reform in 1833 after studying with Mon-affiliated monks. Reformers sought more exact Vinaya observance, standardized Pāli pronunciation, and practices they regarded as textually warranted. Royal patronage gave the smaller order disproportionate institutional influence, and related Dhammayuttika branches later developed in Cambodia and Laos.

## Distinctives and comparative notes

Dhammayut identity is primarily an ordination and administrative affiliation rather than a separate Buddhist doctrine. Monasteries vary in meditation, scholarship, ritual, and relation to forest practice. Although several famous forest masters held Dhammayut ordination, the Thai Forest Tradition is not identical with the order, and many forest communities belong to the majority Mahā Nikāya.
