# Sakya

## Historical context

Khön Könchok Gyalpo founded Sakya Monastery in 1073, building on transmissions associated with the Indian adept Virūpa. The Khön family and ordained masters jointly shaped leadership. Sakya Paṇḍita and Phakpa later played major intellectual and political roles in relations between Tibet and the Mongol empire.

## Distinctives and comparative notes

The Lamdré, or “Path and Fruit,” system integrates Hevajra tantra with a complete account of path and result. Sakya scholarship is renowned for logic, language, and philosophy as well as esoteric practice. Its history demonstrates that Tibetan schools were both contemplative lineages and social institutions; political patronage does not exhaust their religious significance.
