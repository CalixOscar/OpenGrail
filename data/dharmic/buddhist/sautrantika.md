# Sautrāntika

## Historical context

Later Buddhist doxographies use “Sautrāntika” for teachers who challenged Vaibhāṣika Sarvāstivāda orthodoxy and appealed to sūtras over certain Abhidharma claims. Scholars debate whether this was one organized school, several currents, or a retrospective philosophical category.

## Distinctives and comparative notes

Positions associated with Sautrāntikas include representational theories of perception, seeds or latent continuities, and denial that past and future phenomena exist in the same way as present ones. Their arguments shaped Yogācāra and Buddhist logic. The minority tier flags uncertainty about institutional unity, not the existence of the recorded debates.
