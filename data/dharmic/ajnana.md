# Ajñāna Skeptics

## Historical context

Early Buddhist and Jain texts describe skeptical interlocutors, including Sañjaya Belaṭṭhaputta, who declined to affirm or deny major metaphysical propositions. Their own writings do not survive, and rival depictions may stylize their refusal to answer.

## Distinctives and comparative notes

Ajñāna practice appears to have used suspension of judgment in response to questions about afterlife, karma, and the nature of a liberated person. Whether this was a coordinated school or a family of debate strategies remains uncertain. It should not be confused with modern agnosticism despite partial conceptual resemblance.
