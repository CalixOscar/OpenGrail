# Swaminarayan Sampradāya

## Historical context

Sahajānand Swami organized the movement in early nineteenth-century Gujarat and came to be worshipped by followers as Swaminarayan. Temples, monastic leadership, moral codes, vernacular teaching, and social service supported durable communities. Succession produced multiple bodies, including the original diocesan organization and BAPS.

## Distinctives and comparative notes

The tradition combines disciplined Vaiṣṇava devotion, image worship, ethical vows, and reverence for an authorized lineage of teachers. Its branches differ over succession and the theological status of the guru. “Swaminarayan” therefore names a religious family rather than only one contemporary institution.
