# Nāth Tradition

## Historical context

Nāth lineages emerged in medieval South Asia around siddha figures such as Matsyendranāth and Gorakhnāth. Monasteries and itinerant ascetics transmitted yogic disciplines across linguistic and political boundaries, interacting with Śaiva, tantric, Buddhist, and later Sufi settings.

## Distinctives and comparative notes

Nāth practice emphasizes bodily discipline, breath, meditation, mantra, and the transformation associated with *haṭha yoga*. Initiated ascetics are sometimes called split-ear or *kānphaṭā* yogis because of their earrings. The network has never possessed one universal creed, and some communities now function through caste, temple, monastic, or household institutions.
