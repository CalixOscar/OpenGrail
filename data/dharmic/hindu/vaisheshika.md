# Vaiśeṣika

## Historical context

The Vaiśeṣika school is associated with Kaṇāda’s *Vaiśeṣika Sūtra*, composed over time before the early common era. It developed a realist inventory of the world and later converged with Nyāya’s epistemological program. Medieval authors often treated the combined Nyāya–Vaiśeṣika system as a shared philosophical formation.

## Distinctives and comparative notes

Vaiśeṣika classifies reality through categories such as substance, quality, motion, universal, particularity, and inherence. Its account of physical objects is atomistic, but its atoms differ from modern scientific particles and exist within a broader metaphysics including selves, time, space, and mind. Knowledge of these distinctions assists release from suffering. Comparisons with Greek atomism are illuminating only at a general level; no direct historical transmission is established.
