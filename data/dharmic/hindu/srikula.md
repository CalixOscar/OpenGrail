# Śrīkula / Śrīvidyā

## Historical context

Śrīvidyā systems matured in medieval South Asia and became important within South Indian temple, courtly, monastic, and household settings. They synthesize Goddess worship with carefully graded initiation and influential ritual diagrams, especially the Śrīcakra.

## Distinctives and comparative notes

Tripurasundarī is revered as supreme beauty and consciousness, expressed through mantra, the interlocking triangles of the Śrīcakra, and a retinue of divine powers. Ritual may be external, internalized through visualization, or interpreted philosophically. Distinct lineages transmit different mantra sequences and theological frameworks.
