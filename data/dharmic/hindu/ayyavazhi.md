# Ayyavazhi

## Historical context

Ayyavazhi formed in nineteenth-century South Travancore around Ayya Vaikundar and communities confronting severe caste exclusion. Its principal narrative, the *Akilattirattu Ammanai*, presents a sacred history of cosmic ages and Vaikundar’s work against oppressive *Kali*. Worship centers called *pathis* and *nizhal thangals* supported a durable regional network.

## Distinctives and comparative notes

Congregational rites, ethical discipline, shared meals, and rejection of degrading caste practices are prominent. Vaikundar is interpreted through a Vaiṣṇava idiom yet occupies a distinctive revelatory role. Some adherents identify Ayyavazhi as an independent religion, while censuses and scholars often place it within Hindu history. The graph records historical relationship without overruling that living identity debate.
