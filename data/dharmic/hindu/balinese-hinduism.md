# Balinese Hinduism

## Historical context

Balinese religion developed through long interaction among Austronesian ancestral practice, Indic court culture, Śaiva and Buddhist ritual, and the legacy of Java’s Majapahit world. Dutch colonial classification and post-independence Indonesian requirements encouraged institutional articulation as *Agama Hindu Dharma*, but did not create its older temple and village systems.

## Distinctives and comparative notes

Practice coordinates household shrines, temples, priestly rites, offerings, calendrical festivals, irrigation associations, and obligations among gods, humans, ancestors, and place. The ideal *Tri Hita Karana* expresses balanced relations, though its modern prominence has a history. Calling the tradition merely an export of Indian Hinduism overlooks local agency; calling it wholly isolated misses documented Śaiva-Buddhist inheritances.
