# Kashmir Śaivism

## Historical context

Between roughly the eighth and twelfth centuries, Kashmiri authors developed sophisticated Śaiva tantric philosophies and ritual systems. Somānanda, Utpaladeva, Abhinavagupta, and Kṣemarāja are especially influential. Modern usage groups related but distinct currents—Trika, Krama, Spanda, and Pratyabhijñā—under one convenient label.

## Distinctives and comparative notes

These systems often identify ultimate reality with self-aware Śiva-consciousness whose free power manifests the universe. Liberation is recognition of one's identity with that consciousness, cultivated through initiation, contemplation, ritual, and insight. The modern global presentation as a single “Kashmir Shaivism” school can obscure historical differences among texts and lineages.
