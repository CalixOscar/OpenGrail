# Nyāya

## Historical context

Nyāya developed as a rigorous tradition of reasoning, debate, and epistemology around the *Nyāya Sūtra* and successive commentaries. Its authors argued with Buddhists, Jains, Mīmāṃsakas, and other Hindu thinkers. By the medieval period Nyāya increasingly combined with Vaiśeṣika ontology, while Navya-Nyāya created an exact technical language used across disciplines.

## Distinctives and comparative notes

Nyāya analyzes perception, inference, comparison, and trustworthy testimony as means of knowledge. It treats error as diagnosable and liberation as dependent on true cognition of selves and reality. Many later Naiyāyikas defended a creator God through inference, although early texts are less centered on theism. Its debates with Buddhist logic transformed both sides; influence should not be mistaken for agreement.
