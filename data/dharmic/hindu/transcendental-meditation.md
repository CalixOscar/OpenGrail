# Transcendental Meditation Movement

## Historical context

Maharishi Mahesh Yogi began teaching internationally in the late 1950s, adapting a mantra practice rooted in his North Indian monastic lineage for lay audiences. Celebrity interest, teacher training, schools, universities, and research programs helped build a transnational institutional network.

## Distinctives and comparative notes

Practitioners repeat an individually assigned mantra in brief daily sessions. Organizations often describe the technique as natural and nonreligious, while its initiation, lineage, Sanskrit ceremony, metaphysics, and institutional history remain relevant to religious-studies analysis. Claims about exceptional health or social effects require evaluation separately from the movement's documented existence.
