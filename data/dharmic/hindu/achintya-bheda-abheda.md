# Acintya-bhedābheda

## Historical context

The formula of “inconceivable difference and nondifference” summarizes theology systematized by Jīva Gosvāmin and later Gauḍīya authors after Caitanya. It participates in the wider Vedānta debate over how Brahman, individual selves, and the world relate while drawing especially on the *Bhāgavata Purāṇa*.

## Distinctives and comparative notes

Kṛṣṇa and divine energies are inseparable, yet finite selves and the cosmos retain real difference. Their simultaneous unity and plurality exceeds ordinary logical capture and supports a devotional relationship between worshipper and deity. The label is a retrospective doctrinal summary rather than a separate congregational religion.
