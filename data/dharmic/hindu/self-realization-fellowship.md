# Self-Realization Fellowship

## Historical context

Yogananda founded the organization in the United States in 1920 after training in the Yogoda and Kriya Yoga lineage of Sri Yukteswar. Lectures, correspondence lessons, monastic communities, and *Autobiography of a Yogi* made it an influential channel for modern yoga and Vedānta worldwide.

## Distinctives and comparative notes

The movement teaches Kriya Yoga meditation, devotion, ethical discipline, and direct realization of God. Yogananda presented parallels between Krishna's and Jesus's teachings within a universalist theology. Its modern organizational form and transreligious language distinguish it from classical Yoga as a philosophical school.
