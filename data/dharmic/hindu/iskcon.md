# International Society for Krishna Consciousness

## Historical context

A. C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada founded ISKCON in New York in 1966 as a modern institution within Bengali Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇavism. Public chanting, book distribution, temples, communal residences, and vegetarian food programs enabled rapid global growth. After Prabhupada’s death, governance and leadership developed through a collective commission amid reforms and controversies.

## Distinctives and comparative notes

ISKCON centers devotion to Kṛṣṇa through congregational chanting of the Hare Krishna mantra, deity worship, scriptural study, ethical vows, and service. It teaches Caitanya as a divine descent and emphasizes disciplined *bhakti*. Its countercultural Western history is only one chapter of a transnational community now substantially rooted in India and diverse diasporas.
