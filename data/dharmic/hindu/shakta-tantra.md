# Śākta Tantra

## Historical context

Goddess-centered tantric scriptures and lineages became visible in the early medieval period within a broader Śaiva tantric world. They developed across regions and courts, monasteries, temples, and household settings. Later systems such as Śrīvidyā and Kālīkula transformed earlier ritual repertoires in different ways.

## Distinctives and comparative notes

Initiation, mantra, mandala or yantra, visualization, deity installation, and the ritual body organize many Śākta tantric paths. Some texts prescribe transgressive substances or rites, while many living traditions use highly regulated symbolic or temple forms. “Tantra” is neither a single religion nor a synonym for sexuality; it names diverse textual and initiatory systems.
