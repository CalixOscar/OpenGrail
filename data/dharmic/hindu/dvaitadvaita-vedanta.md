# Dvaitādvaita Vedānta

## Historical context

The Nimbārka lineage developed a form of *bhedābheda* Vedānta, usually dated to the late first or early second millennium CE. Later communities linked its metaphysics to devotion toward Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa and organized the Nimbārka Sampradāya in North India.

## Distinctives and comparative notes

Souls and matter depend completely on Brahman and are therefore non-different, yet possess real qualities and agency that make them different. The relation is compared to sun and rays. Dvaitādvaita is one among several difference-and-nondifference systems and should not be conflated with Gauḍīya Acintya-bhedābheda.
