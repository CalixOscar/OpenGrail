# Smārta Tradition

## Historical context

“Smārta” has referred broadly to Brahmins oriented toward *smṛti* and domestic Vedic norms, and more narrowly to communities associated with Advaita Vedānta and Śaṅkara-linked monastic centers. The familiar five-shrine *pañcāyatana* pattern honors Śiva, Viṣṇu, Devī, Sūrya, and Gaṇeśa without requiring exclusive allegiance.

## Distinctives and comparative notes

Smārta practice combines household rites, temple worship, life-cycle observances, and philosophical study. Its inclusive deity arrangement is sometimes presented as a universal template for Hinduism, but it represents one historical formation among many. Advaita supplies an influential account of divine forms within nondual reality, yet not every Smārta practitioner is primarily philosophical, and multi-deity worship also occurs outside Smārta institutions.
