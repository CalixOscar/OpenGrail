# Cārvāka / Lokāyata

## Historical context

Indian philosophical literature repeatedly describes materialists called Cārvākas or Lokāyatas, but most supposed foundational texts are lost and surviving portraits are often polemical. The labels may cover multiple skeptical and naturalistic currents rather than one continuous school.

## Distinctives and comparative notes

Positions attributed to Cārvāka include prioritizing perception, denying disembodied souls and postmortem karma, and criticizing priestly revelation. Familiar slogans about crude hedonism may caricature opponents. It belongs in the atlas as an intellectual current within South Asian religious debate, not as a well-documented modern denomination.
