# Code of Conduct

OpenGrail maps religions, mythologies, and ethical philosophies, including living
traditions that people hold sacred and traditions that people have left. That makes the
tone of this project part of its substance.

## The standard

- **Describe, do not adjudicate.** Record that a tradition makes a claim; do not assert
  the claim is true or false. The `epistemic_tier` and relation `certainty` fields exist
  precisely so contributors never have to argue about whose faith is correct.
- **Scholarly and neutral.** No proselytizing, no apologetics, no polemics, and no
  dismissive language about any tradition or its adherents.
- **Argue from sources.** Disagreements about lineage, dates, or geography are settled by
  citation, not by conviction or volume.
- **Assume good faith** in reviews and issues, and give correction without contempt.

## Not accepted

Harassment, slurs, or demeaning remarks about religion, ethnicity, nationality, gender,
sexuality, disability, or any other personal characteristic; sustained disruption of
discussions; sectarian point-scoring; personal attacks; publishing others' private
information; and using the issue tracker or pull requests as a platform for religious or
anti-religious campaigning.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages, and data
contributions in this repository.

## Enforcement

Report conduct problems to the maintainer at **peter@calmdownoscar.com**, or by reporting
the content to GitHub. Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer may edit,
lock, or delete contributions and may block accounts. Responses are proportionate:
usually a warning first, and a block for repeated or severe behavior.
